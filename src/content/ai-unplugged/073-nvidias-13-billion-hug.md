## 🗞️ AI News of The Week: Nvidia to Acquire Hugging Face for $13 Billion 🤗

Nvidia is reportedly moving to buy Hugging Face, the open model repository nearly every AI developer relies on, for $12.9 billion.

Hugging Face works like GitHub for AI models: developers and researchers search for models that fit their needs, download and run them, fine-tune them, and upload the new versions back for others to use.

**Why this matters:**
- **Infrastructure grab:** Hugging Face has become the default place to store and download open-weight models, giving Nvidia real influence over that ecosystem
- **Hedge against frontier labs:** OpenAI, Anthropic, and others are increasingly building their own custom chips, so owning Hugging Face gives Nvidia a way to keep the open-model side of the industry leaning on its hardware
- **A second shot at AI cloud:** Nvidia has struggled to get its own cloud AI business off the ground, and this could help restart that effort
- **Reach into robotics:** Hugging Face has been investing in models for robotics and other physical AI applications, an area where Nvidia is already a major player

## 🧠 AI Term of The Week: Dogfooding 🐶

**Definition:**
Dogfooding is when a company uses its own product internally, in real work, before or while selling it to customers.

**Explain Like I'm 5:**
It's like a chef tasting their own cooking before it goes out to the table.

**Why it matters now:**
- AI tools change so fast that using them yourself, daily, is often the only way to catch what's actually broken
- Companies that dogfood tend to ship fixes faster because they feel the pain first
- If a company won't run its own business on its own AI tool, be wary

## 🧰 AI Tool of The Week: Handy 👋

![Handy speak-into-any-text-field landing page with a Google Docs voice dictation demo](/assets/media/2026/08/handy.png)

Handy is a free, open source speech-to-text app that lets you dictate straight into any text field on your computer.

**Why I use it:**
- Honestly, probably because of the fun, squishy logo, but it's also a good, free, open-source alternative to [WisprFlow](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/flow), which is awesome, but not free.

**What it does:**
- Turns your voice into text in any app, not just one specific chat window
- Runs quietly in the background, triggered with a hotkey whenever you want to talk instead of type
- Free and open source, no subscription required
