## 🧠 AI Term of The Week: Clanker

**Definition:**
Clanker is internet slang for AI, robots, or bots, borrowed from Star Wars (where it was a nickname for battle droids) and now used online as a casual, often dismissive way to call something out as AI.

**Explain Like I'm 5:**
It's like calling a robot "tin can," a joking nickname people use instead of just saying "AI" or "bot."

## 🧰 AI Tool of The Week: Veed.io

Veed is a browser-based AI video editor built for creators who want polished video without learning traditional editing software.

**What it does:**
- Auto-generates subtitles and captions in dozens of languages
- Turns a script into a talking-head video using AI avatars and voice cloning
- Repurposes long videos into short, social-ready clips
- Runs entirely in the browser, nothing to install
- Lowers the skill bar for creators

👉 [Check out Veed.io](https://www.veed.io/)
