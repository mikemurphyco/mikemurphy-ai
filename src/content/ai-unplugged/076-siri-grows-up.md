## 🧠 AI Term of The Week: Grounding

**Definition:**
Linking an AI's answers to real, verifiable source documents to make sure it tells the truth.

**Explain Like I'm 5:**
It's like making a student show their work and cite the textbook page instead of just guessing at the answer.

**Why it matters now:**
- Hallucinations are still the biggest trust problem in AI, and grounding is the main fix
- More tools now cite their sources by default, not as an afterthought
- For creators and businesses, grounded AI means fewer embarrassing made-up facts in client-facing content

## 🧰 AI Tool of The Week: Jev by TypeSafe AI

Jev is TypeSafe AI's first "System One Model," built to make fast, reliable decisions inside software and automated workflows instead of just chatting with a human.

**Why it's interesting:**
- It's a different shape of AI model, built for machines calling machines instead of humans typing prompts
- Points to where a lot of AI infrastructure is heading
- Useful for builders who want AI judgment inside a pipeline without the overhead of a full chatbot

**Real-world example:**
A workflow tool could call Jev to decide whether an incoming support ticket needs a human, instead of routing every ticket through a full LLM conversation.
