## 🧠 AI Term of The Week: WorkSlop

**Definition:**
Workslop is a slang term for useless, sloppy, or annoying AI output that creates more work instead of less. It's used in the workplace when a co-worker gives you a document that was clearly written by AI, and the only way to understand it is to give it to an AI to translate and simplify.

**Explain Like I'm 5:**
Imagine asking a robot to clean your room, but instead of putting your toys away, it just dumps a giant pile of mismatched blocks and dirty socks in the middle of the floor and calls it done.
