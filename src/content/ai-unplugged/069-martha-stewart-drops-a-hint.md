## 🧠 AI Term of The Week: Technical Debt

**Definition:**
Tech debt is the extra work, cost, or friction created by past technology decisions that no longer serve you well.

**Explain It Like I'm 5:**
It is like keeping a closet full of things you rarely use because you might need them someday. Eventually, finding anything becomes harder than replacing what you kept.

**Real examples:**
- Paying for Airtable because one old workflow still lives there
- Keeping a Notion workspace because it contains a few useful notes
- Maintaining an automation nobody fully understands
- Using three AI tools that mostly do the same thing
- Avoiding an update because it might break an old setup

**Why it matters now:**
- AI makes it easy to add new tools, workflows, agents, databases, and subscriptions. The difficult part is removing them.
- For solo builders, tech debt can quietly consume time and attention. You spend more energy maintaining yesterday's setup and less time building what matters next.
