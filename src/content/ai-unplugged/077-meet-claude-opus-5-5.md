## 🗞️ AI News of The Week: Anthropic Introduces Claude Opus 5.5

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22, with improvements to writing, coding, and the cost of running AI workflows.

Anthropic says the new model produces clearer responses, follows writing instructions more closely, and handles complex work more efficiently. The company also reports lower operating costs than Opus 5 and higher usage limits for paid Claude subscribers.

**Why this matters:**
- **Clearer writing:** Anthropic says Opus 5.5 puts key information first and uses less jargon. Try it on a draft you already know well.
- **More room to work:** Five-hour usage limits are increasing on Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans.
- **Cheaper automated workflows:** Anthropic reports typical API workloads cost about 40% less than Opus 5 in its tests. That is an operating-cost claim, not a subscription discount.
- **A useful comparison:** Run an existing assignment through the new model and check whether it needs fewer corrections.

👉 [Read More About Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)

## 🧠 AI Term of The Week: Opaque Recurrence

**Definition:**
Opaque recurrence is when an AI model repeatedly processes internal information without expressing each intermediate step in readable words.

**Explain Like I'm 5:**
Imagine someone working through a puzzle on a hidden scratchpad, then showing you the answer.

## 🧰 AI Tool of The Week: Google Vids

Google Vids is a video creation and editing tool for people who want to make explainers, training videos, and presentations with AI assistance.

**Why it's interesting:**
- Turns Google Slides presentations into video projects and provides templates and stock media.
- Records your screen and audio, with AI voiceovers and avatars also available.
- Lets collaborators edit, comment, and view through sharing controls.
- Create and edit on desktop. Advanced features and usage limits depend on your plan.

👉 [Explore Google Vids](https://workspace.google.com/products/vids/)

## 🛠 AI Tutorial Of The Week: How To Build Your First AI Agent From Scratch

**Description:**
Build a simple Evergreen agent with two Markdown text files and Claude Code in VS Code, then give it the job of recommending your next article.
