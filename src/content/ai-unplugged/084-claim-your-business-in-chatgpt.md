## 🗞️ AI News of The Week: Businesses in ChatGPT

You can now claim your business in ChatGPT.

OpenAI has launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta. Business owners use a new tool called Business Hub to claim their profile and keep their information current. To prove you own the business, you verify it with a business phone number, email address, website domain, or Shopify account.

Business Hub also shows how people find you in ChatGPT: how often you're mentioned, how many people click to your website, and the topics that come up in those conversations. Eligible U.S. online stores and service businesses can also publish a free Business Agent that answers customer questions.

OpenAI says that claiming a profile does not guarantee better placement in ChatGPT's answers.

👉 [Read Article](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)

## 🧠 AI Term of The Week: Runbook

**Definition:**
A runbook is a written, step-by-step set of instructions for a task you do again and again, so a person or an AI agent can do it the same way every time.

**Explain Like I'm 5:**
A **runbook** is a step-by-step recipe book for computers and websites. Just like a recipe tells you how to bake a cake, a runbook tells tech workers how to fix a broken computer or keep a website running smoothly.

**Real examples:**
- A creator writes a runbook for publishing a video, and an AI agent follows it to make the description, chapters, and blog post.

## 🧰 AI Tool of The Week: Nod by n8n

**What is Nod:**
Nod is a free, open-source personal AI agent from n8n for busy people who want help with everyday errands on real websites and apps.

**What it does:**
- Works across Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion, and the web with its own computer and browser.
- Asks for your approval before it sends a message or makes a change for you.
- Runs on your own machine with the AI model you choose, including a ChatGPT Plus or Pro sign-in.

**Real-world example:** Every morning, Nod reads your messages, calendar, and the client's latest news, then writes a one-page brief for each call.

Nod is a research preview for Mac, with Windows and Linux in preview.

👉 [Download Nod on GitHub](https://github.com/n8n-io/nod)
