## What Is the Amazon Associates Program?

Started in 1996, Amazon Associates is the largest and most successful online affiliate program, with close to a million members. It's free to join and open to anyone with a website, an Amazon seller account, or web development skills. Amazon provides specially formatted links for products you want to promote; when someone clicks your link and buys something on Amazon, you earn a commission.

**Commission rates at the time (typically 0-10%, averaging 4-8%):**

- 0% — digital downloads (music, video, software, games, eDocs, Kindle books), gift cards, and wine
- 1% — video games and consoles
- 2% — televisions
- 2.5% — computers, computer parts, DVDs and Blu-ray
- 3% — toys
- 4% — Fire tablets and Kindles
- 4.5% — physical books, health and personal care, automotive, baby products
- 6% — headphones, beauty, musical instruments, business and industrial supplies
- 7% — clothing, smart TVs, jewelry, luggage, shoes, handbags, watches, Echo and Alexa devices
- 8% — furniture, home, lawn and garden, pets
- 10% — Amazon Fashion (women's, men's, kids private label), luxury beauty, Amazon Coins
- 4% — everything else (electronics, gear, cameras, video)

## Bounties: Flat-Rate Payouts

Amazon also runs promotions paying flat "Bounty" amounts, which can add up with enough traffic. Examples at the time: Audible trial memberships ($5), Audible Gold memberships ($10), Amazon Prime referrals ($3), Amazon Fresh groceries ($5), Kindle memberships ($5), and Twitch Prime ($3).

## How to Join

Joining is easy and free — apply directly through the [Amazon Associates program](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/). <!-- REVIEW: original link included third-party tracking params (ascsubtag=1794169...) that don't look like Mike's own affiliate link; replaced with the plain program URL rather than propagate what may be someone else's tracking tag -->

## Pros of Amazon Associates

Passive income, a steady revenue source, and no traffic restrictions — new site owners with little traffic can join. One side tip: if you're just getting your online presence off the ground, hold off joining until you have some momentum, since long stretches of inactivity can get an account closed.

- People already trust Amazon, so they're comfortable clicking affiliate links.
- **24-hour cookies** — affiliates get credit for any purchase made within 24 hours of a click, not just the specific product linked.
- Amazon offers plenty of promotional tools.
- It's motivating — money might be the initial driver, but over time the focus naturally shifts to making genuinely helpful content.

## Policies

See the full [Amazon Associates Operating Agreement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement). Things that can get an account banned:

1. Shopping through your own affiliate links.
2. Putting affiliate links in email newsletters or PDFs.
3. Putting affiliate links in eBooks.
4. Opening multiple accounts — use tracking IDs for multiple websites instead.
5. Skipping the required disclaimer.

## Ways to Use Amazon Affiliate Links

- Create content around products, gear, and tools in your niche.
- Gear, tech, and product reviews.
- Blog posts, tutorials, and articles.
- Podcast show notes on your website.
- Book reviews and favorites.
- Resource pages on your site.
- A curated gear list. <!-- REVIEW: original linked to kit.co/mikemurphyco (Kit.com) which shut down in 2023 — link dropped -->

## In Conclusion

The Amazon Associates program is a solid way to teach people about products you actually use and believe in — and most people feel fine using affiliate links from someone they know and trust, since it costs them nothing. Follow the rules carefully, since a simple mistake can get an account shut down.
