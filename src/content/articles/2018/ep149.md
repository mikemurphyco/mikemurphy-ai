**Episode Summary:** An announcement episode: after months of decluttering physical and digital life, Mike Murphy Unplugged goes on hiatus after Episode 150 — and why letting go isn't the same as giving up.

## What You'll Learn

- The announcement: Mike Murphy Unplugged was going on hiatus after Episode 150
- How months of aggressive decluttering, physical and digital, led to this decision
- What "one man band" really means to Mike: freedom and independence, not working alone
- The benefits of letting go, and why it isn't the same as quitting

> "The secret to happiness is freedom... and the secret to freedom is courage." — Thucydides

## The Announcement

After months of aggressively purging and decluttering his physical and digital environments, and taking honest inventory of his business and life, Mike came to a difficult realization: this podcast had become a weight, not a lift. Stubbornness could have kept it going forever, but that wouldn't have been healthy. Letting go isn't the same as quitting — it just makes room for something new.

**Mike Murphy Unplugged is going on summer hiatus.** The pause takes effect after Episode 150. This isn't the end of podcasting for Mike, just a break. Since launching in September 2015, he'd published a new episode every Friday morning at 6:00 AM without fail — the podcast helped him find his voice as a creative and gave him a platform to teach. He's extremely proud of the body of work built, and it's time to start a new chapter.

## Backstory

Mike moved from Colorado to Montana to Portland to New Hampshire and back to Colorado, traveling everywhere in between, often living out of a Subaru wagon or VW van. The freedom and independence of that life came largely from not owning much — being debt-free and unencumbered let him pick up and go whenever opportunity knocked.

**"One man band"** is a phrase Mike uses often, and it represents exactly that: the ability to move and act on his own terms, without being tied down. Simplifying and cutting back to essentials is about maintaining control over what matters most, and eliminating what doesn't serve his goals. The less he has, the better he feels.

## The Minimalist Process

For several months, Mike turned decluttering into a kind of game — thinning out everything, even the small stuff that doesn't seem to take up much room. Gear and tools that weren't used regularly went to be given away or sold. Kitchen cupboards, junk drawers, bathroom cabinets, closets — all pared down to only what actually gets used. The same approach applied digitally: iPhone home screens went from 10 to 2.5, hard drives and Google Drives got cleaned and properly backed up, unused Mac apps got deleted, and email subscriptions and old accounts got ruthlessly cut. Anything unused is one less notification, one less distraction.

## The Benefits of Letting Go

Feeling lighter, cleaner, and freer. Less stuff means feeling swifter and more nimble, with reduced stress from actually knowing what you own. A clearer, more focused mind. More motivation and productivity. A few hundred dollars made selling things along the way, and less desire to buy anything new. Maybe the biggest benefit: the process of simplifying belongings carries over into everything else, helping you appreciate the small things and prioritize what actually matters.

## My Why

Creating the life Mike wants means being free to live on his own terms. The difference now is building a business to support that financially, rather than relying on jobs working for others. The why behind Mike Murphy LLC and Murphy Concierge — the daily grind of building an online business and brand — is freedom: creative and financial freedom as the vision of success in business, and the freedom to travel and do what inspires him as the vision of success in life.

None of this was expected to be easy — trial and error, ups and downs, pivots along the way were always part of the plan. Recognizing that part of the content machine had started introducing negativity, rather than fighting through it for the wrong reasons, Mike chose to make a change. Pressing pause on Mike Murphy Unplugged wasn't an easy decision, but it's the right one for now. This isn't quitting — it's evolving, and moving forward on the road to freedom and happiness.
