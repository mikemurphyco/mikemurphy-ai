**Episode Summary:** A mid-year check-in on content strategy and work-life balance ahead of a 2-week vacation to Nantucket — planning the second half of 2018 and reflecting on the value of taking a real break.

## What You'll Learn

- A mid-year review framework for evaluating your life and business roadmap
- Why podcasting didn't make the 2018 priority list, but stayed important anyway
- The 2018 mantra: Simplicity, Fundamentals, Storytelling
- Micro-storytelling as a teaching approach — breaking big concepts into small pieces
- Priorities for Q3 and Q4 2018 across Murphy Concierge, YouTube, writing, and health

> "The mind should be allowed some relaxation, that it may return to its work all the better for the rest." — Seneca

## Taking a Break

Resting your mind is vital to your health and wellness, but that doesn't mean stopping your efforts to create the life you want. Mike's sights were on the long-term — a marathon, not a sprint — so pace mattered. July 18th marks the halfway point of the year, a natural moment to evaluate a life and business roadmap and set up a strong finish. Are you on the right track? Do you know where you're headed? Are you in control, or in reaction mode?

Mike shared in [Episode 149: The Freedom Show](/podcast/ep149/) that he was taking an indefinite hiatus from the podcast. This episode plans the content strategy and business goals for Q3 and Q4 of 2018 ahead of a 2-week vacation to Nantucket — his version of work-life balance.

## The 2018 Roadmap Revisited

In [Episode 122: The 2018 Roadmap Show](/podcast/ep122/), the year's priorities were: Murphy Concierge (generating income), Tutorials & Classes, Writing, Ask Mike Murphy, YouTube, and Health & Wellness.

Looking back at the halfway mark, Mike felt good about progress against that roadmap — with one note: podcasting didn't make the original priority list, but remained important to the overall content puzzle, especially as audio and podcasting kept growing in a mobile-first world. The plan wasn't to walk away from podcasting, just to find a new rhythm — a step back to take two steps forward.

**Mantra and theme for 2018:** Simplicity, Fundamentals, Storytelling — maintaining a lifestyle of reduced physical and mental clutter, with more emphasis on fundamentals and storytelling in content creation.

## Storytelling on the Trip

Mike approached the Nantucket trip with storytelling in mind — drawing from personal experience in a way others could relate to and connect with, rather than worrying about volume of content. The goal: **micro-storytelling** — taking big concepts and breaking them into smaller pieces instead of trying to cover too much at once, so that snippets of the journey add up to a larger story. Ideas on the table included behind-the-scenes looks at gear and workspace, the "why" behind the work, and glimpses into life and business — not to self-promote, but to let people into the journey.

## Priorities for Q3 and Q4 2018

- **Murphy Concierge** — Mike's local tech support business, helping homes and businesses fix and understand their tech. Homework: build a membership subscription plan giving clients ongoing access to help.
- **Tutorials, YouTube & Ask Mike Murphy** — growing the YouTube channel significantly, plus adding a livestream Ask Mike Murphy component to actually meet and engage the audience.
- **Writing** — doubling down on Medium, with a goal of at least one more eBook for the year and mining 150 podcast episodes for 50-100 article topics.
- **Health & Wellness** — treated as the true #1 priority: the belief that taking care of the body makes everything else fall into place.

## Bottom Line

Taking a step back and taking a break does the body and mind good. Creating the life you want is non-stop hard work, but it's vital to block out time to clear the board, think, and re-evaluate. Checking in with yourself is essential.

> "You can discover more about a person in an hour of play than you can in a year of conversation." — Plato
