## What You'll Learn

- Why LinkedIn was becoming more of a content-creator platform in 2018
- Key LinkedIn facts and demographics at the time
- Expert advice from livestreaming pioneer Ross Brand on where to focus first on LinkedIn

This is part 1 of a 3-part social media series with special guest Ross Brand from [LivestreamUniverse.com](https://livestreamuniverse.com), covering LinkedIn, Twitter ([Episode 126](/podcast/ep126/)), and Facebook Live ([Episode 127](/podcast/ep127/)) for content creators, personal brands, and online businesses.

## About LinkedIn

LinkedIn is a professional networking platform geared toward job seekers and business connections, built around profiles that function as resumes or bios. Tagline: "Relationships Matter." Founded by Reid Hoffman in 2002, launched in 2003.

> "If you aren't embarrassed by the first version of your product, you shipped too late." — Reid Hoffman

That echoes one of Mike's own favorite principles: done is better than perfect. Just start.

**Facts about LinkedIn at the time:** LinkedIn acquired Lynda.com in 2015 for $1.5 billion; Microsoft acquired LinkedIn itself for $26.2 billion in 2016. 530 million users, 70% outside the US, 40 million students and recent grads, 57% male / 44% female, 41% of millionaires use it, the average CEO has 930 connections, 5.5 million accountants are on the platform, 44% of users earn over $75,000/year, and 3 million active job listings. Profiles with professional headshots get 14x more views and 36x more messages. The most overused profile word, then and for years prior: "Motivated." <!-- REVIEW: LinkedIn user counts, demographics, and acquisition-era stats are from 2018 and are now significantly outdated -->

## Q&A With Ross Brand

**Why should creators use LinkedIn in 2018?** LinkedIn was shifting toward more of a content-creator platform with the addition of video, and relatively few creatives were using it that way yet — meaning it was easier to stand out.

**What should someone focus on first?** The profile itself — that's where to put initial effort, whether starting fresh or updating an existing account.

**What content strategy works, and how often should you post?** Share your best material related to your niche or industry, and post about once a day.
