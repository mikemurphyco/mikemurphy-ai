**Episode Summary:** Mike's annual roadmap episode — the mantra and theme for 2018 (fundamentals and storytelling), the 6 priorities for Mike Murphy LLC, and the content schedule he's committing to for the year.

## What You'll Learn

- Mike's mantra and theme for 2018: fundamentals and storytelling
- The 6-priority roadmap for Mike Murphy LLC, from Murphy Concierge to health and wellness
- The 2018 content schedule across podcast, tutorials, articles, and social
- Why Mike writes a public roadmap every year instead of keeping goals private

**Roadmap quote:** *"If you don't know where you are going, any road will get you there."* — paraphrased from Lewis Carroll's *Alice in Wonderland*, from the dialogue between Alice and the Cheshire Cat about which way to go when you don't know where you want to end up.

Creating a roadmap forces real thinking about objectives and aspirations, and putting a plan in place makes some of them a reality. It won't be followed perfectly, but it gives clarity on the general direction — a follow-up to last week's [2017 recap in Episode 121](/podcast/ep121/).

## Mantra & Theme for 2018: Fundamentals and Storytelling

2017 was about simplicity and reducing physical and mental clutter — that stays in effect. 2018 adds **fundamentals and storytelling**: who am I, what am I doing, why am I doing it, how am I doing it. The goal is a blueprint of the journey that helps hit big-picture goals while helping others create the life they want through the content being made.

Sharing a real origin story matters because people trust and buy from brands with a backstory they can relate to. The catch-phrase driving all of it: **Create the Life You Want.**

## The 2018 Mike Murphy LLC Roadmap

1. **Murphy Concierge (generating income).** The side hustle launched at the end of 2017 started strong and becomes the main business focus for 2018 — the goal is for it to cover the bills and create some breathing room.
2. **Tutorials & classes.** Teaching and educational content stay core to the personal brand, but with more intention and focus than 2017. The plan: teach for the beginner mind and bundle tutorials into fundamental packages or classes to sell.
3. **The One Man Band Book.** The 2017 goal to write it didn't happen — trying again in 2018, with the podcast playing a role in developing chapters, possibly shared in audio form as episodes depending on response.
4. **Writing (beyond the book).** Repurposing existing content — tutorials, posts, podcast episodes — into tighter articles for the online portfolio. Writing is a daily habit already; the goal is tightening the packaging and message.
5. **Ask Mike Murphy.** A Q&A format to build authority and personal brand further, likely via livestreams on Facebook and YouTube.
6. **YouTube.** Finding a groove and growing a real community there, plus generating income through AdSense and Amazon Affiliate product mentions.
7. **Health & wellness.** Treated as foundational to everything else — physical and mental health as the base the entrepreneurial and creative work rests on. More content planned around routines and this part of the journey.

The throughline: Murphy Concierge generates problems to solve, those problems become tutorial content, tutorials bundle into packages, and the process gets documented in articles and videos — all while staying healthy enough to sustain it.

## The 2018 Content Schedule

- **Podcast & newsletter:** 1 per week
- **Tutorials & quick tips:** 3 per week
- **Medium.com articles:** 2 per week
- **Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn:** 5 days per week
- **Instagram Stories:** 7 days per week
- **Livestream:** 1 per week
- **Products:** 2 step-by-step bundled classes for sale by year-end
- **Book:** in draft form by July 2018
- **Merch:** 1 signature t-shirt
