**Episode Summary:** A behind-the-scenes look at Mike's self-imposed writing challenge, the "Write the Life" project — publish a self-published eBook by April 15, 2018 — plus a quick overview of eBooks in general.

## What You'll Learn

- A quick overview of eBooks: what they are, where to buy them, and typical pricing
- The self-imposed "Write the Life" eBook challenge: publish by April 15, 2018
- Why Mike chose eBooks as the next content format after podcasting and video
- The vision behind building a passive-income content platform through self-published books

**37 days remaining** in the challenge at the time of recording.

## What Is an eBook?

An eBook — electronic book — is a digital book made of text, images, or both, readable on computers, phones, or tablets. The digital counterpart to a printed book.

## Benefits of eBooks

Generally inexpensive, an endless catalog to choose from, and convenient to read on a phone, tablet, or dedicated e-reader like the Kindle. eBooks let you highlight, bookmark, add notes, and search text — and take up zero luggage space.

## Where to Buy Them

Amazon, Apple Books, Google Play Books, Barnes & Noble, and Kobo, among others — though Amazon and the Kindle Store dominate the market and make self-publishing highly accessible. Amazon was Mike's primary focus, with distribution to smaller sellers planned too.

## Pricing

Generally under $10, with the most common Kindle Store price points around $2-3.99, and plenty of free or $0.99 titles.

## Purchasing and Reading

Dedicated readers like Kindle or Nook allow direct purchases on-device. On iPhone or iPad, purchases happen through Amazon first, then sync easily to the free Kindle app — Mike's own preferred way to read, primarily on an iPad Pro.

## What Was WritetheLife.com?

<!-- REVIEW: writethelife.com was an abandoned project — confirmed dead, intentionally left unlinked -->

A new blog built specifically to force a public commitment: publish an eBook on Amazon by April 15, 2018. The reasoning leaned on Parkinson's Law — work expands to fill the time you give it — the same logic that got this podcast launched in 30 days with zero prior podcasting or audio-recording experience. The challenge: self-publish an eBook and document the whole process, tools included, on the blog, with a companion 5-10 episode podcast series and a class planned alongside it.

## Why Start This Project?

The broader vision for Mike Murphy LLC is educational and motivational content teaching creatives the tech and lessons learned building an online business — audio, video, and words are the three legs of that content stool, and eBooks fit naturally into the "words" leg. Writing has always been a strength (an English degree helps), and there's been a long-simmering, semi-secret desire to make it as a blogger that never got pursued seriously after an early attempt. This project doubles as a forcing function — a deadline to finally ship something that's been weighing on him — and a realistic one, given 131 podcast episodes' worth of source material (roughly 3,000 words each) to draw from for eBooks targeting around 10,000 words apiece.

## Vision and Long-Term Goals

The goal: build a platform of tutorials and educational content that generates passive income through products and affiliate links. eBooks work well as marketing products too — brands can use them almost like a business card — and Mike wants to make the self-publishing process (blog, podcast, social channels, and the book itself) as approachable as possible for others.

## Conclusion

The goal: eBook #1 live in the Kindle Store by April 15, 2018, with progress documented along the way and an open invitation for anyone who wants to join the challenge.
