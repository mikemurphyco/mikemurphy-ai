## The Instagram Stories Show

**History lesson:** Facebook bought Instagram in 2012 for $1 billion. A year later, in 2013, Facebook tried to buy Snapchat for $3 billion — Snapchat said no. Snapchat is now valued at over $20 billion, and Facebook (now valued at over $350 billion) simply built its own version and called it Instagram Stories — nearly identical to Snapchat in format.

### How to Use Instagram Stories

Two quick video walkthroughs: [How to Use Instagram Stories](https://youtu.be/GyxVBXHTRGA) and [How to Add Your Camera Roll to Instagram Stories](https://youtu.be/yhEBu48d7lI).

### Instagram vs. Snapchat

**Instagram:** easier to build an audience on, since it starts from an existing follower base. **Snapchat:** takes a lot more work to grow from scratch.

### Benefits of Using Instagram Stories

Reach (building an audience), networking, brand awareness, marketing products and services, establishing authority, and teaching/helping others.

### Ideas to Get Started

Be yourself. Show behind the scenes of a business or workflow. Teach. Share genuinely valuable tips. Be a person of value — authentic, genuine, real. Don't overthink it — keep it simple.
