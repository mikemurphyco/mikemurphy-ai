## 5 Benefits of Being a Guest on a Podcast

1. **Build authority.** Position yourself as an expert in front of a new audience.
2. **Grow your own audience and listener base.** Most podcast listeners subscribe to multiple shows, so gaining new listeners with every interview is likely — especially on shows covering similar ground.
3. **Networking.** A guest appearance grows a professional rolodex fast.
4. **Confidence & communication skills.** The more you get out there talking about yourself and your brand, the more confident and skilled you become at it. A genuinely underrated benefit.
5. **Revenue.** [Episode 28](/podcast/ep28/) covered just how expensive running a podcast can be — guest interviewing is a great way to generate leads and sell products and services. A good reminder that having actual products and services to sell is worth prioritizing.

## 5 Tips to Prepare to Be a Guest on a Podcast

1. **Be prepared.** Enough said.
2. **Organize your assets.** Make it easy for the host and audience to spread the word about you.
3. **Handle the tech.** Get everything ready ahead of time and confirm it works.
4. **Be professional.** Dress for success and represent your brand properly, even on audio.
5. **Listen and show gratitude.** Just be yourself, and be genuinely nice.
