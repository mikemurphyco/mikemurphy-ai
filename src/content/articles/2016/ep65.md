## What Is Adobe Premiere Pro CC?

[Adobe Premiere Pro CC](https://goo.gl/ZRM418) is the video editing tool bundled into Adobe Creative Cloud — powerful enough for full-length films, simple enough for a casual family-vacation edit.

**Non-linear editing (NLE):** Premiere is a non-destructive editor — it never touches the original source files, so quality is always preserved regardless of how many edits get made.

## Alternatives to Premiere Pro

[iMovie](https://www.apple.com/imovie/), [Final Cut Pro X](https://www.apple.com/final-cut-pro/), [Avid Media Composer](https://www.avid.com/media-composer), [Blackmagic DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve), [Windows Movie Maker](https://www.videowinsoft.com/movie-maker).

**Screencasting software some use as a primary editor:** [ScreenFlow](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/screenflow), [Camtasia](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/camtasia).

**Bonus tips:** every verified YouTube channel has a feature-rich video editor built right into Creator Studio — an entire video can be assembled there. [Filmora](http://filmora.wondershare.com/) is also a solid, inexpensive online editor at $50/year.

## How Much Does Premiere Pro CC Cost?

$19.99/month for the à la carte option, or $49.99/month as part of the full Adobe CC bundle.

## Other Adobe Products Used for Video

- **[Photoshop](http://www.adobe.com/Photoshop)** — for animated GIFs and simple animations (Photoshop's video handling has genuinely improved a lot).
- **[After Effects](http://www.adobe.com/products/aftereffects.html)** — for more complex animations, like the Podshed Gift Guides. The same program used on Star Wars and countless other Hollywood productions.
- **[Premiere Pro](https://goo.gl/ZRM418)** — the home for any video mixing camera footage and stills, including every tutorial, with Photoshop and After Effects integrated as needed.

## Is It Recommended for Beginners?

Absolutely. Premiere is powerful and feature-rich, but not complicated once the interface and workflow click. Getting genuinely good at editing and effects takes time — same as any new skill worth learning.

## Verdict

A big thumbs up for Adobe Premiere Pro CC.
