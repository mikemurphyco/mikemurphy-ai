- How email and domain aliases work for a podcast business
- 4 concrete ways podcasters can use Google Apps day to day

## Ask Mike Anything: Do You Need Expensive Headphones?

**Question:** Are expensive headphones necessary for recording a podcast, or are Apple earbuds good enough?

**Answer:** Upgrading from Apple earbuds is worth it if possible — dedicated headphones give a flatter, cleaner sound that makes it much easier to catch recording issues. **Tip:** still keep a pair of Apple earbuds around for quality control, since a lot of listeners hear the show through them — knowing how it sounds on common devices matters.

## What Is Google Apps for Work?

Per Google's own description: an all-in-one suite to communicate, store, and create. [Google Apps](https://apps.google.com/) covers the admin side of a business — email, calendar, word processing, spreadsheets, and more. Not the flashy stuff, but the stuff that keeps a business on track.

## What Does It Cost?

$5/user per month for the base plan, or $10/user per month for increased storage and additional features.

## What's Included with Google Apps

**Communication apps:** [Gmail](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/gmail/), [Calendar](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/calendar/), [Google Hangouts](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/hangouts/), and Google+. **Storage:** [Google Drive](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/drive/). **Collaboration tools:** [Google Docs](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/docs/), Sheets, Forms, Slides, and Sites — see Google's own [collaboration tips](https://apps.google.com/learning-center/tips/collaborate-better/#/list).

**Tip — [email aliases](https://support.google.com/domains/answer/6304345?hl=en):** create unlimited prefixes on one domain — mike@, info@, hello@, feedback@, etc.

**Tip — [domain aliases](https://support.google.com/a/answer/53295?hl=en):** route other owned domains through the same account, so an address like mike@askmikeanything.com works too.

[Gmail](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/gmail/), [Calendar](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/calendar/), [Google Drive](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/drive/), [Google Docs](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/docs/) (Docs, Sheets, Forms, Slides), and Google+.

## Ideas for Podcasters

1. **[Google Sheets](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/sheets/) (or Excel).** Build a master episode list with notes and topics covered, plus resource lists of products, tools, and links.
2. **[Google Forms](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/forms/).** Create surveys, feedback forms, or audience contests (voting on a new feature, for example). **Also worth checking out:** [Wufoo](http://wufoo.com) and [IFTTT](https://ifttt.com/).
3. **[Collaboration & sharing](https://apps.google.com/learning-center/tips/collaborate-better/#/list).** Cuts out email back-and-forth, works well for coaching and client consulting, lets pre-interview questions get sent via Google Forms, supports co-host collaboration on scripts or show notes, and gives virtual assistants a shared workspace.
4. **[Google Slides](https://apps.google.com/intx/en_us/products/slides/).** Build keynote-style presentations and screencasts.
