**Episode Summary:** The Official Unofficial 1st Annual Mike Murphy Unplugged/PodcasterToolkit/PodShed/PodGift Holiday Ideas Guide — gear, big-ticket items, books, and unique gift sources for podcasters and creators.

## What You'll Learn

- The first-ever Mike Murphy Unplugged holiday gift guide, gear through big-ticket items
- A curated book list for podcasters and entrepreneurs
- Where to find handmade and unique gifts online
- A Tim Ferriss-inspired lifestyle gift category, from coffee to clothing

**Tip:** mobile podcasting and livestreaming gear makes a strong gift category for the podcaster or content creator on your list — along with clothing and props for video.
