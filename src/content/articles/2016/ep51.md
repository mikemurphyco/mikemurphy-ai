**Episode Summary:** Writing for podcasters and content creators — the writing process, strategy, and the exact tools used to get words out of the head and onto the page.

## What You'll Learn

- The 8 types of writing a podcaster or content creator regularly does
- Mike's 4-tool writing stack: Google Drive, Scrivener, Byword, and Workflowy
- How Byword's multi-markdown shortcuts map to standard heading levels

Got a favorite writing resource or tool worth sharing? Send it to mike@mikemurphy.co.

## Writing Examples for Podcasters

Podcast outlines and scripts, show notes, marketing promos, newsletters, emails and correspondence, tutorials and teaching content, blog posts and articles, and eBooks/guides/books.

## Tools

1. **[Google Drive](http://drive.google.com)** — Docs, Sheets, Slides, for general writing and collaboration.
2. **[Scrivener](http://mikemurphy.co/scrivener)** <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass --> — the primary writing tool of choice.
3. **[Byword](http://bywordapp.com)** — a $12 Mac App Store app built around multi-markdown, ideal for show notes. Heading shortcuts map directly: `#` = H1, `##` = H2, `###` = H3, `####` = H4.
4. **[Workflowy](http://workflowy.com)** — for building outlines.
