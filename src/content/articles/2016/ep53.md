## What Is Livestreaming?

Simply real-time video recording broadcast over the internet — whether from an ESPN studio or an iPhone held up on a beach.

## 5 Popular Livestreaming Services (2016)

<!-- REVIEW: livestreaming platform landscape below is from 2016 — Google Hangouts on Air, Firetalk, and standalone Periscope have all changed dramatically or shut down since -->

- [Periscope](https://www.periscope.tv/) — owned by Twitter, released in 2015.
- [Google Hangouts on Air](https://hangouts.google.com/start) — now part of YouTube.
- [Firetalk TV](https://firetalk.com/mike-murphy-co) — home of "Open Mike Night: Ask A Podcaster Anything," every Monday at 9pm ET.

## Topic Ideas

**Tip:** come prepared with an outline or bullet points.

- **Tutorials** — teach and share knowledge; builds trust and authority.
- **Q&A sessions** — an "Ask Mike Anything" format works well for networking and genuine helpfulness.
- **Behind the scenes** — people consistently want to see how the work actually happens.
- **Promotion & marketing** — podcasts, products, services, courses.
- **Practice first** — Instagram Stories, Snapchat, and vlogging all make good low-pressure warmups before jumping into full livestreaming.
