## Ask Mike Anything: LED Lighting for Video

**Question:** Is LED lighting recommended for video, and why?

**Answer:** Yes. **Pros:** lightweight, stays cool, low power consumption, great accessories, easy to use. **Cons:** often more expensive, can carry color tints, not as bright, and not always as flattering as tungsten or fluorescent kits.

**What to look for when buying LED lights:** a CRI (Color Rendering Index) above 90, and daylight-balanced output (5600K). **General recommendation for any camera/video gear:** call B&H, describe the budget, and let them recommend specific lights.

**LED lights used here:** [Ikan IFD576-S Daylight LED Light](http://astore.amazon.com/videounplu-20/detail/B00HEGP0C6), [Fotodiox Pro LED-312DS](http://astore.amazon.com/videounplu-20/detail/B00FL7LLZA), [Genaray LED-6200T 144 LED Light](http://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/857246-REG/genaray_led_6200t_144_lamp_variable_clr.html). Also worth a look: Film Riot's review of [Aputure Amaran LEDs](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/fotodiox).

## 5 Types of Video to Start Making Today

**Benefits for podcasters:** engagement with listeners, established authority, teaching, sharing knowledge, showing authenticity and vulnerability, building trust, promoting the podcast and individual episodes, and marketing products or services.

1. **Behind the scenes.** Show off the workspace or podcast setup.
2. **About Me video.** A promo video introducing yourself to listeners and the wider community.
3. **Gear reviews & product demonstrations.** Unboxings or demos of a microphone, mixer, or anything else — good gear reviews can lead to affiliate income or brands sending free products.
4. **Tutorials & screencasting.** The easiest way to start making video, and a good entry point for anyone who'd rather not be on camera. Software options: [Screenflow](https://www.telestream.net/screenflow.htm) by Telestream, or [Camtasia](https://www.techsmith.com/camtasia.html) by TechSmith.
5. **Vlogging.** Get personal with day-in-the-life videos, uploaded to YouTube, Snapchat, or Instagram, letting the audience see daily routines. Not for the faint of heart, but a great way to build trust and a loyal following.

**Bonus idea:** trailers for individual podcast episodes — [Sean McCabe does a great job with this](https://youtu.be/WBhFDvziVQE).
