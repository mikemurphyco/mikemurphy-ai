**Episode Summary:** Content is king, but the real leverage comes from repurposing — 10 concrete ways to turn one podcast episode into social posts, newsletters, articles, webinars, books, and more.

## What You'll Learn

- Why repurposing matters more than creating even more new content
- 10 concrete ways to turn one podcast episode into many pieces of content
- The social media automation tools used to keep episodes circulating
- A useful WordPress plugin for scheduling posts and show notes

## Ask Mike Anything: Scheduling Blog Posts and Show Notes in WordPress

**Question (from Blab):** Is there a way to schedule blog posts and show notes in WordPress?

**Answer:** One favorite plugin: [WP Editorial Calendar](https://wordpress.org/plugins/editorial-calendar/) — free, and drop-dead simple to use. It gives a more visual way to schedule draft posts in WordPress, speeding up the blogging workflow considerably.

## What Is Repurposing Content?

Taking anything created — a podcast, blog post, or behind-the-scenes video — and either posting it across different platforms, apps, or channels, or slicing and dicing it into multiple fresh pieces. **Why repurpose?** A lot of time and effort goes into making one piece of good content. A single tweet or blog post doesn't give that work the exposure it deserves — content is created to provide value, so it should actually get in front of people. That's what repurposing accomplishes.

## 10 Ways to Repurpose Podcast Episode Content

1. **Social media.** Share each episode's content repeatedly and creatively to build authority and exposure. **Tools:** [Buffer](https://buffer.com/) (free and excellent), [Tweet Jukebox](http://www.tweetjukebox.com/) (automates and recycles tweets, free plan available), [MeetEdgar](http://meetedgar.com/) (powerful Twitter automation, $50/month).
2. **Newsletter.** Email is the best communication channel with an audience — share episode content there in a variety of formats. Be innovative, and show the audience why the content was created through guides, infographics, or other unique framing.
3. **Blab/Periscope/live streaming.** Embed the video and reuse the audio from live streams on YouTube and the website. Spread it around.
4. **Blog post.** Beyond good show notes for each episode, individual concepts from an episode can become standalone blog posts — the work is already done, just repackaged.
5. **Medium article.** [Medium](http://medium.com) is a favorite internet magazine and a great way to build authority as a thought leader — turn podcast content into a white paper or guide.
6. **Webinar.** Webinars or Google Hangouts make a great evergreen promotional platform — for example, a webinar on repurposing content that demonstrates turning one episode into a lead-magnet guide. It functions like a reusable class, and doubles as a promo tool for a paid course since most of the content is already done. **Resource mentioned:** [WebinarNinja](http://webinarninja.co).
7. **Kindle book/audiobook.** Bundling episodes together into a book is a genuine goal here. **Books mentioned:** [#AskGaryVee](https://www.youtube.com/hashtag/askgaryvee) by Gary Vaynerchuk (a compilation of his podcast) and [The School of Greatness](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-school-of-greatness/id596047499?uo=4) by Lewis Howes (also built from his podcast). *Book links are affiliate links — a small percentage comes back on any purchase.*
8. **Audio apps.** Two favorites: [Clammr](http://clammr.com) and [Anchor](http://anchor.fm). With Clammr, clip a short teaser from each new episode and post it to Twitter and Facebook. With Anchor, record a quick question or thought-provoking snippet drawn from the episode.
9. **Lead magnets.** Turn episode content into guides, infographics, checklists, or resources to grow an email list.
10. **Tutorials & classes.** Bundle several episodes into a class sellable on [Udemy](http://udemy.com) or [Skillshare](http://skillshare.com), or break them into bite-sized standalone tutorials.
