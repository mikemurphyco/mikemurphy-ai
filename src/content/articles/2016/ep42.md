**Episode Summary:** A behind-the-scenes look at the full 10-step podcast production workflow — from verifying an episode went live through newsletter curation for the next one.

## What You'll Learn

- The full 10-step production workflow, start to finish, for every episode
- Why exercise and breakfast made the list as a real workflow step
- The tools used at each stage: writing, recording, tagging, and scheduling
- How social media promotion gets batched around the recording day

## The 10-Step Workflow

1. **Verify.** Confirm the new episode is live in iTunes and in the Podcasts app. **Tip:** subscribe to your own show — it makes it easy to catch typos or swap a bad audio file quickly.
2. **Organize & set up the framework.** Set up the WordPress blog post, create episode artwork in InDesign, and replicate the newsletter template in Mailchimp.
3. **Social media marketing.** Use [Buffer](http://bufferapp.com) in Chrome to queue posts across Facebook, Twitter, and Google+.
4. **Exercise & breakfast.** A genuinely important part of the workflow — not a throwaway step.
5. **Script writing.** See [Episode 40](/podcast/ep40/) for the full scripting process. The [Pomodoro Timer App](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/pomodoro-timer-focus-on-your/id872515009?mt=12) ($2.99) helps keep writing sessions focused.
6. **Social media maintenance & correspondence.** Re-buffer the morning's posts and respond to comments, replies, and likes.
7. **Recording & editing.** Done in [Adobe Audition](http://audition.adobe.com).
8. **Tagging & uploading.** Tag the MP3 with [ID3 Editor](http://www.pa-software.com/id3editor/) <!-- REVIEW: pa-software.com — small/old third-party tool, verify still active during the link review pass --> and upload to [Libsyn](http://libsyn.com) <!-- REVIEW: historical hosting reference — the podcast is hosted on Buzzsprout today, not Libsyn -->.
9. **Show notes & WordPress scheduling.** Show notes get drafted partly in [Scrivener](http://mikemurphy.co/scrivener) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->, mostly in [Byword](https://bywordapp.com/), a multi-markdown writing app.
10. **Newsletter & topic curation.** Handled in [Mailchimp](http://mailchimp.com).
