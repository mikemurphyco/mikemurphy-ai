## What You'll Learn

- Why this episode was recorded on-location at Baker Beach in San Francisco
- The full travel and location-recording gear list, packed and priced

- The 3 biggest challenges of mobile/location recording

**Today's episode is Part 1** of a series on how to record a podcast on location. [Part 2](/podcast/ep50/) and [Part 3](/podcast/ep52/) continue the series.

## Why Record in San Francisco?

**Reason 1:** to learn the ins and outs — practice makes perfect. **Reason 2:** mobile podcasting and live streaming/broadcasting are the future. **Reason 3:** one of the "whys" from [Episode 22: The Why Show](/podcast/ep22/) was to eventually run a location-independent business — traveling and podcasting from interesting locations sounds like a great way to do that. No rush, but it's coming in one form or another.

## Why Record on Location?

Impromptu interviews or man-on-the-street style pieces at events, trade shows, or meetups; CEO interviews at their own location (or any business person, really); even RV travel, someday.
