**Episode Summary:** What Adobe Creative Cloud actually is, what it costs, and every Adobe program used across the podcast production workflow — from audio editing to episode artwork to video.

## What You'll Learn

- What Adobe Creative Cloud actually is, and what it costs at every tier
- The exact Adobe program used for every part of the production workflow
- A list of the most useful Adobe mobile apps at the time
- Why routine, not motivation, is what actually keeps a publishing schedule consistent

## Ask Mike Anything: Staying Consistent with a Busy Lifestyle

**Question:** How do you keep a consistent publishing schedule with a busy lifestyle?

**Answer:** The podcast has to become a priority and part of the routine — no exceptions. **Bottom line:** it needs to sit on the calendar like any other commitment. If the current format isn't working or the podcast starts controlling you, adapt and change it. This show runs as a one-man-band format because it fits — interviews would likely drive more downloads, but doing what genuinely fits and sticking to a schedule is what actually keeps episodes on time.

## What Does Adobe Creative Cloud Cost?

A [free 30-day trial](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/desktop-app.html) is available. Individual subscriptions run $50/month; students and teachers get the full bundle for just $19.99/month. À la carte single programs (like Audition) run $19.99/month. The Photography Bundle (Photoshop + Lightroom) is just $9.99/month.

## A Quick Overview of Adobe CC

Adobe Creative Cloud bundles 28 professional-grade programs and roughly 12-15 mobile apps. It also integrates with [Behance](https://www.behance.net) (online portfolios), [Adobe Stock](https://stock.adobe.com) (stock photography), and [Typekit](https://typekit.co/foundries/adobe) (font library). **Places to learn Adobe tools:** [Lynda.com](http://www.lynda.com), [KelbyOne](http://www.kelbyone.com), [Skillshare](http://skillshare.com).

## The Adobe Tools Actually Used

**Podcast audio editing:** [Adobe Audition](https://www.adobe.com/products/audition.html). **Photography and general workflow:** [Photoshop](http://www.adobe.com/Photoshop), [Lightroom](http://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop-lightroom.html), [Bridge](http://www.adobe.com/products/bridge.html). **Episode artwork and publishing work (PDF guides, etc.):** [InDesign](http://www.adobe.com/products/indesign.html). **Logo and vector work:** [Illustrator](https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator.html). **Video work:** [Premiere Pro](https://www.adobe.com/PremierePro), [After Effects](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects.html), [Adobe Media Encoder](https://www.adobe.com/products/media-encoder.html), and Speedgrade. **Other tools:** Adobe Acrobat and Adobe Muse.
