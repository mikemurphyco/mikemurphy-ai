## Ask Mike Anything: Recording Interviews Beyond Skype

**Guest question from:** Cara Lumen, [Deeper Song Community](http://passionatelyonpurpose.com/deeper-song-community).

**Question:** Any suggestions for recording coaching calls or interviews besides Skype?

**Answer:** Plenty of options — [Zoom](http://zoom.us/), [Google Hangouts](https://hangouts.google.com), or iOS apps like [Ringr](http://www.ringr.us). The pick, though, is a newer service called [Zencastr](http://zencastr.com), whose tagline is "record your guests in top quality directly from their web browser." It's built specifically with podcasters in mind — send a link to guests, hit record, and Zencastr handles the heavy lifting, delivering high-quality audio on separate tracks. Genuinely easy.

## I. Why Start a Podcast?

**Why:** the purpose, cause, or belief that drives what you do. **Resource mentioned:** Simon Sinek's *Start with Why*.

**What inspired this podcast?** The purpose is simply to teach and inspire people to do great things by sharing technical knowledge and a unique perspective.

The belief driving it comes from a Steve Jobs quote: *"We're here to put a dent in the universe. Otherwise why else even be here?"* It may be naive, but the belief holds that talent, skills, interests, and a genuine passion to learn and create things can add up to something meaningful. A recurring frustration with being a jack-of-all-trades has always come from knowing there's a lot to offer and wanting to share it and help others along the way — wanting to put a dent in the universe, no matter how big or small, without ever being quite sure how to start.

The core "why" here: this podcast is a platform to teach and impact others, and a starting attempt at making that dent. Pretty deep, admittedly — but knowing your why is genuinely critical, since it's what keeps you motivated, focused, and excited when the work gets hard.

## II. Why Start a Podcast in 2016?

There are real benefits to podcasting for one-man bands and small brands. **Disclaimer:** podcasting is a big commitment that takes real work to start and maintain — it's a great platform, but that shouldn't be downplayed.

<!-- REVIEW: original WordPress source numbered this list "10 reasons" but the source text itself only contains 9 numbered items (jumps from 5 to 7, no distinct #6) — preserved as-is rather than inventing a 10th reason -->


1. **It's gaining popularity.** Still a relatively new medium, so getting in early helps establish long-term footing.
2. **Entertain and be creative.** It's your show — make it great.
3. **Establish authority.** Be seen as an expert in your field or topic.
4. **Networking.** Meet and cross-promote with other podcasters, and with guests if interviews are part of the format.
5. **Relationships.** Engaging an audience builds real connections — community is how great businesses get built.
6. **Global audience.** The potential reach extends worldwide.
7. **Inexpensive.** Not free, but the investment is minimal and attainable for most people.
8. **Market & promote your brand.** Use the podcast as a communication and marketing tool.
9. **Make money.** A podcast alone rarely generates much income directly, but it can drive side income through affiliate links and promoting products and services.
