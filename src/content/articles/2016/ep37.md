**Episode Summary:** What Instagram is, how it fits into a real photo and editing workflow, and 9 concrete tips for podcasters looking to promote their show through it.

## What You'll Learn

- Quick Instagram FAQ: history, user base, and photo/video specs
- The full photo and photo-editing workflow used for Instagram
- A go-to hashtag set and how to compile your own
- 9 concrete Instagram tips built specifically for podcasters

## Ask Mike Anything: Voice & Audio Apps for Promotion

Today's question came via email from Vivian Carrasco of [The Turning Inward](https://goo.gl/1o5W2B) podcast: **Question:** what's the difference between all the voice apps like Anchor and Clammr, and which ones are worth using?

**Answer:** Two favorites for podcast marketing and promotion: [Clammr](http://www.clammr.com/) (24-second soundbites) and Adobe Voice — since renamed [Adobe Spark Video](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/adobe-spark-video-easily-create/id852555131?mt=8) (as of May 2016), which mixes in photos and video.

## Instagram FAQ

- Started in 2010
- A photo and video social media app for iOS and Android
- 400 million active users, up from 90 million just 3 years earlier
- Bought by Facebook for $1 billion in 2012
- 29% of the US population over 12 uses Instagram
- More than 80 million photos uploaded daily
- 3.5 billion daily likes
- Photos no longer have to be square — any aspect ratio is permitted
- Videos up to 1 minute long

## Why Instagram Gets Used Here

Marketing, personal creativity and development, networking, and simply a love of photography.

## How Instagram Gets Used

**Workflow:** shoot on a [Canon T5i](http://goo.gl/h2GriO), edit in Adobe Lightroom and Adobe Photoshop, and use [AirDrop to move photos to an iPhone](https://youtu.be/BF3uyN6ZVJw) for final posting.

**Related iOS apps:** Adobe Post — since renamed [Adobe Spark Post](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/adobe-spark-post-create-stunning/id1051937863?mt=8) — plus [Over](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/over/id535811906?mt=8), [Boomerang](http://blog.instagram.com/post/131684343987/boomerang-from-instagram) and [Hyperlapse](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/hyperlapse-from-instagram/id740146917?mt=8) (both by Instagram), [VSCO](https://vsco.co/store/app) (popular), [Snapseed](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/snapseed/id439438619?mt=8) (a great photo editor), and [PicsArt](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/picsart-photo-studio-all-in/id587366035?mt=8).

**Photo tip 1:** export photos to Instagram at 2048x2048px. **Photo tip 2:** the Dehaze filter in Lightroom and Camera Raw is the secret sauce. **Done is better than perfect:** don't obsess over editing every Instagram photo.

## Hashtags

Look at what others in the niche use and compile a personal set. [This text-replacement shortcut](https://youtu.be/_tJCz19Ie9s) makes adding hashtags fast. **Main hashtag set used here:** #learn, #create, #moveforward, #unplugged, #onemanband, #podcaster, #podcasters, #podcasts, #howtopodcast, #podcastlife, #podcasting, #audio, #gear.

## Engage

Search for people, search by clicking hashtags, and genuinely like, comment, and say hello to people.

## Instagram Tips for Podcasters

1. Post regularly and stay consistent with look and style
2. Use the right hashtags
3. Be social — follow people, like photos, leave comments
4. Use video — Snapchat, Boomerang, Hyperlapse, or any format
5. Promote each episode on release day; tag guests and ask them to promote too
6. Use Instagram to find future guests
7. Interact with hosts of shows worth being a guest on
8. Cross-promote across other channels
9. [Run Instagram contests](http://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/run-successful-instagram-contest/)
