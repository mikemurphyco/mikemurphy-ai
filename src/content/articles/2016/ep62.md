**Episode Summary:** What Medium.com is, how it actually works, and 5 concrete ways it can help a podcaster or content creator build authority and exposure.

## What You'll Learn

- What Medium.com actually is: part blog, part social network, part self-publishing platform
- The mechanics: how recommending, responding, and publications work
- 5 concrete ways Medium can benefit a podcaster or content creator
- Why Medium is a genuinely good place to start writing a book, chapter by chapter

## What Is Medium?

A site where anyone can write articles of any length on any topic — a genuine hybrid of blog, social media channel, online magazine, and self-publishing platform, popular with both internet personalities and major brands.

## Facts & Figures

Launched in 2012 by Ev Williams (co-founder of both Twitter and Blogger). Unlike Twitter's character limits, Medium has no writing length cap, and pulls in an estimated 40–50 million monthly visitors.

## How to Use It

Log in with a Twitter or Facebook ID, customize the profile and bio, click "New Story" and start writing. A small **+** icon appears in blank space for adding photos; pasting a YouTube URL anywhere embeds the video automatically. Standard text formatting — headlines, block quotes, lists, bold, italic — is all supported by highlighting selected text.

## The Social Side

Clicking the heart icon "recommends" a story (Medium's version of a like) and turns it green — the author gets notified (can be disabled). Responding to a story surfaces that response on your own main feed. Other features include bookmarking favorites and creating publications (essentially a personal, curated magazine). Popular topics skew heavily toward tech, design, entrepreneurship, and self-development/lifestyle content.

## Why Use Medium

It's become a genuine daily reading habit — subscribing to topics of interest delivers articles straight to the inbox. It's also a place to build writing muscle, gain brand exposure, and establish real authority around podcasting and content creation.

## How Medium Can Benefit a Podcaster or Content Creator

Medium functions like any other social channel for promotion and marketing, but with a more thoughtful, "expert-in-the-room" feel — a genuinely strong place to stand out in a specific topic area.

1. **More exposure for existing content.** Write directly on Medium, or (common approach) keep the primary blog elsewhere and cross-post with a link back — quick to do, and gets content in front of more eyes. Imagery and video matter here since Medium is a visual platform.
2. **Start a Medium publication.** A way to curate a specialized "newspaper" around a specific niche — genuinely useful for building brand authority and collaborating with others.
3. **Teach.** Medium handles tutorials, how-to guides, and embedded video well — top-10 lists and "ultimate guides" perform especially well, so it's a natural place to flex teaching skills.
4. **Network.** Recommend and respond to other writers' work frequently.
5. **Write a book.** Genuinely — several writers have published entire books chapter by chapter directly on Medium. For anyone with a book goal, it's a legitimately good place to start.
