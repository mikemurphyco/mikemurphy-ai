- A repeatable pre-recording checklist for on-location podcasting
- The Auphonic-to-Dropbox-to-Audition post-production pipeline

## The Podcaster's Toolkit: Product of the Day

[Sugru](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/sugru) — $12 for a 3-pack, $22 for 6. Essentially moldable super glue that sticks to nearly any surface, mainly for fixing and mending, with plenty of creative hacks beyond that. [Some Sugru uses via Lifehacker](http://lifehacker.com/top-10-diy-miracles-you-can-accomplish-with-sugru-756259927). Real-world use here: repairing and reinforcing Apple power cables before they split, and molding a hook to hang [Sony MDR-7506 headphones](https://www.mikemurphy.co/sonymdr7506) on the side of a desk. The individual packets are about the size of a to-go mustard packet — handy to keep around the studio or in a travel bag.

## Post-Production

Export from Auphonic to Dropbox, import on the studio computer, edit in Adobe Audition.

## The Beach-Cast Checklist

- Confirm the iPhone has enough storage for audio and behind-the-scenes footage.
- Set the iPhone to Airplane Mode.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb.
- Charge the iPhone to full.
- Bring backup USB portable chargers and cords.
- Bring stands or tripods.
- Turn on the ATR2100.
- Check input levels in Auphonic (aim for -18dB to -9dB).
- Be prepared (water, snacks).
- Test the recording before going live.
- Record. Have fun.
