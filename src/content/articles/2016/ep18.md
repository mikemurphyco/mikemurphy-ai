## What You'll Learn

- A brief recap of the 2016 Roadmap, goal by goal, with real actions attached
- The exact tools used to build the roadmap and stay on track all year
- A recommended lavalier mic setup for better iPhone/Periscope audio
- Two great external resources for running your own annual review

**[Download the 2016 Roadmap PDF](https://mikemurphy.ai/assets/media/2016/01/2016_Roadmap.pdf)** — see [Episode 17](/podcast/ep17/) for the full roadmap this episode recaps.

## Ask Mike Anything: Better Audio for iPhone Periscope

**Question:** How do you get better audio when using an iPhone for Periscope?

**Answer:** The Rode SmartLav+ is the recommended microphone — it works with all iOS devices.

![Rode Smartlav+](/assets/media/2015/11/IMG_8920-150x150.jpg)

[Rode SmartLav+ Lavalier Microphone](http://astore.amazon.com/videounplu-20/detail/B00EO4A7L0).

**Recommended accessories:** the [Rode SC3 3.5mm TRRS to TRS Adapter](http://astore.amazon.com/videounplu-20/detail/B00L6C8PNU) (for use with other devices — cameras, the Zoom H6), the [Rode VXLR Adapter](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/rodevxlr), and the [Rode SC6 Dual TRRS Input & Headphone Output for Smartphones](http://astore.amazon.com/videounplu-20/detail/B00KHQW6JU).

## Priorities of 2016

- **Goal:** improve podcast quality and take audio production skills to the next level. **Action:** ask for feedback and help in Google+ groups, study, and practice.
- **Goal:** make more video and written tutorials, and improve video skills. **Action:** commit to one video tutorial per week, and keep studying video editing.
- **Goal:** increase networking and build relationships in the podcast community. **Action:** serving hours on social media, plus direct contact.
- **Goal:** drive more website traffic and grow the email list. **Action:** increase the number of lead magnets and calls to action.
- **Goal:** repurpose content more consistently. **Action:** turn 2015 episodes into blog posts and tutorials, and reuse Blab content.
- **Goal:** monetize. **Action:** more product videos for affiliate links and courses.

## Social Media Plan

Twitter: 5 tweets/week. Facebook: 2 posts/week. Instagram: 3-5 posts/week. Pinterest: 1/month. Blab: Open Mike Night, Mondays at 9pm EST. Serving hours: 2 hours/week.
