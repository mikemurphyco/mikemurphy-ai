## What You'll Learn

- The 7 concrete steps to actually launch a podcast, in order
- Realistic launch timelines: 4-6 weeks solo, 8-10 weeks minimum for interview shows
- 5 podcasts and courses worth studying to learn the craft
- A tool for promoting episodes with short audio teasers

> "Perfection is not attainable, but if you chase perfection, you can catch excellence." — Vince Lombardi

## Ask Mike Anything: Promoting with Audio Teasers

**Guest question from:** Dr. Vivian Carrasco, [The Turning Inward Podcast](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/turning-inward-dr.-vivian/id961459563?mt=2).

**Question:** Are there tools for promoting a podcast with audio teasers?

**Answer:** Yes — [Clammr](http://www.clammr.com/), essentially "Twitter for audio." It lets 24 seconds of audio get shared on any social media channel or website, a great way to promote individual episodes.

## How to Start a Podcast: 7 Steps

> "You can't build a reputation on what you are going to do."

### 1. Pick a Launch Date

4-6 weeks is a reasonable timeline for a one-man-band show, 8-10 weeks minimum for an interview format. **Action step:** put the launch date on Google Calendar and hold yourself to it.

### 2. Create a Master Document

Organize and plan as thoroughly as possible. **Action step:** create a Google master doc and write everything down.

### 3. Learn Your Craft

**Resources mentioned:** Dave Jackson's [The School of Podcasting](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/school-podcastings-morning/id83653087?mt=2), Daniel J. Lewis's [The Audacity to Podcast](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/audacity-to-podcast-how-to/id378425347?mt=2), Ray Ortega's [The Podcasters' Studio](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/podcasters-studio-how-to-record/id312884767?mt=2), Cliff Ravenscraft's [The Podcast Answer Man](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/podcast-answer-man-podcasting/id208529334?mt=2), Pat Flynn's [How to Start a Podcast Tutorial](http://www.smartpassiveincome.com/how-to-start-a-podcast-podcasting-tutorial/), and John Lee Dumas's [Free Podcast Course](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/free-podcast-course/id947671706?mt=2).

### 4. Define Your Topic & Content

What's genuinely exciting to talk about? Go there.

### 5. Choose a Format

One-man band? Interview show? Co-host? Storyteller? See [Episode 21: The Format Show](/podcast/ep21/) for a full breakdown of each.

### 6. Commit to a Publishing Schedule

Daily? Weekly? Monthly? Pick a schedule and stick to it — audiences want consistency above almost everything else.

### 7. Define Long-Term Goals — for You and Your Audience

What should the podcast do for you? What should it do for your audience? Is this a path to making a living, or is it 100% a hobby?
