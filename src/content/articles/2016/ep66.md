## What Is Adobe Creative Cloud?

[Adobe Creative Cloud](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud) bundles creative apps like Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, and Audition together. **Pricing:** $49.99/month for the full bundle, $19.99/month à la carte, or $9.99/month for the [Photographer Bundle](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography.html) (Photoshop, Lightroom, Bridge). Education discounts are also available.

## CC Libraries

[Adobe Creative Cloud Libraries](https://goo.gl/uh4lra) function like a Dropbox for creative assets, shared across every Adobe app. Instead of hunting down a logo or brand colors each time, everything — photos, vectors, brushes, patterns, color swatches — lives in one folder accessible from Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, and beyond. A genuine time saver, and the entry point into working from mobile devices too.

## Adobe Mobile Apps

**Main categories:** Design & Illustration, Video/Visual Storytelling, and Photography & Creative Imaging.

**Two standalone apps to start with:** the **Creative Cloud app** (access to every library and everything made in the mobile apps) and **Adobe Capture** — genuinely remarkable: photograph anything and turn it into a vector illustration, texture, or pattern, ready to drop straight into Illustrator.

### Design & Illustration

Three main apps, two in heavy regular use:

- **Adobe Draw** — used for drawing every product image featured in videos. A favorite.
- **Photoshop Sketch** — a painting and sketching app with an extensive brush/marker/pencil library, similar in spirit to Procreate. **Side tip:** anyone into digital painting should check out [Kyle Webster Brushes](https://gumroad.com/kyletwebster).
- **Adobe Comp** — useful for InDesign work and web layout, not used daily but genuinely impressive when it comes up.

### Video/Animation

- **Adobe Spark Page** — builds a full story webpage from supplied text, images, and video in minutes. A podcast intro page, for instance, is realistically achievable in under 10 minutes.
- **Adobe Spark Post** — similar in spirit to Canva: strong typography and pre-built templates for text/quote graphics, shareable directly to social.
- **Adobe Spark Video** — animated video stories from templates or custom builds, using supplied text and images.
- **Adobe Premiere Clip** — covered previously in [Episode 65](/podcast/ep65/), it enables full video creation/editing right on iPhone or iPad — soundtrack and transitions included, in minutes.

### Photo Apps

Adobe Lightroom Mobile, Photoshop Mix, and Photoshop Fix.
