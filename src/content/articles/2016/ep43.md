## What You'll Learn

- 5 areas every one-man-band business should have an emergency plan for
- A real 3-step backup strategy: cloud, local, and off-site
- How to tell if someone on Snapchat follows back
- Why a rainy-day fund matters enough to batch-record episodes around it

> Failing to plan is planning to fail.

## Ask Mike Anything: Does Someone Follow You Back on Snapchat?

**Question:** Is there a way to tell if someone being followed on Snapchat is following back?

**Answer:** Yes, two tells: 1) if a Chat option appears when flipping up from someone's story, they're following back; 2) a visible SnapScore on their profile also confirms it.

## The Emergency Toolkit: 5 Areas to Prepare

1. Protecting your gear & security
2. Backup plan
3. Asset management / inventory
4. Password management
5. Banking

### 1. Security

[The Canary Surveillance Camera](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/canary) and [Mr. Beams Motion Detector LED Lights](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/mrbeams).

### 2. Backup Plan

**Step 1:** start with Google Drive and Dropbox for documents and small essentials carried around for years — tax records, PDFs, driver's license, passport, health insurance card.

**Step 2:** two local external hard drives, with Time Machine running on one of them. LaCie and Other World Computing are favorites; Western Digital and G-Technology drives have also worked well.

**Step 3:** cloud storage via [Backblaze](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/backblaze). Other cloud storage options: [Crashplan](http://crashplan.com), [Carbonite](http://carbonite.com).

### 3. Inventory (Asset Management)

The [Sortly](http://www.sortlyapp.com/) iPhone app.

### 4. Password Management

[1Password](https://1password.com/) or [LastPass](https://lastpass.com/).

### 5. Banking

A rainy-day savings plan, and batch-recording episodes ahead of time as a buffer against the unexpected.
