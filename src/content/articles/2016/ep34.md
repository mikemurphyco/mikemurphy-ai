## The Snapchat 101 Overview

What is Snapchat? How is it used? How do you build an audience on it?

**Quick facts:** a social media platform and image-messaging app, launched in 2011 by 3 Stanford students. The company is valued around $20 billion. Snapchat sees 7 billion video plays per day, versus Facebook's 8 billion — a genuinely big player in social media. Gary Vaynerchuk is a vocal fan. The logo's name is "Ghostface Chillah" — a Wu-Tang reference.

### 1. How Do You Use Snapchat?

Available on iOS and Android. Every photo or video message sent is called a Snap, running 1-10 seconds long. Snaps disappear once viewed, though they can also be saved.

**The Story (Snapchat's foundation):** Snaps get sent as private messages or added to a Story. Story content stays visible for 24 hours before disappearing. Viewers of a Story are visible (and vice versa) — treat the Story as a canvas, stay creative, and leave people wanting more.

### 2. The Creative Toolkit

1. **Drawing tools & text overlays.** Draw in color or add scalable, colorable text.
2. **Stickers.** Graphic icons and emoticons that can be scaled, rotated, and repositioned.
3. **Filters.** Overlay effects — Instagram-style color grades, black & white, clock, speed, temperature overlays.
4. **Geofilters.** Location-dependent custom graphics, submittable for free or purchasable as ads.
5. **Lenses.** Face-recognition graphics and overlays for humor and fun.

### 3. How Do You Add Friends?

1. **Add by username** — mine is MikeMurphyBTS ("behind the scenes").
2. **Add from address book** — the app searches phone contacts.
3. **Add by Snapcode** — the yellow graphic with a white ghost (sometimes an animated GIF inside); take a picture of it to add. **Note:** screenshotting someone's Snapcode notifies them.
4. **Add nearby** — add friends physically close by, at a coffee shop or event.

**Resource mentioned:** Ghost Codes, an app for finding Snapchat users by interest tag (e.g., "podcasters").
