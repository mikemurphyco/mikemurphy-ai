**Episode Summary:** A behind-the-scenes look at building PodcasterToolkit.co — why it exists, the steps taken to go from idea to live site, and the monetization plan behind it.

## What You'll Learn

- Why PodcasterToolkit.co exists and what it was originally meant to be
- The full launch checklist: hosting, theme, domain, branding, and photo organization
- The affiliate monetization plan behind the site
- Why starting beats endless planning, every time

**Got a favorite tool or resource?** Send it to mike@mikemurphy.co to get added to the kit. Vendors and product creators are also welcome to reach out for testing consideration, as long as it's genuinely useful to listeners.

## This Week's Podcaster's Toolkit Pick

The [Zoom H4n Pro](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/h4n) — an audio classic. A handy portable recorder that accepts 4 XLR microphones, with a built-in stereo mic on top for when nothing else is plugged in. Records to a memory card, and doubles as an audio interface. A high-confidence recommendation.

## Why PodcasterToolkit.co Exists

**A quick history lesson:** the whole reason for starting Mike Murphy Unplugged was to share knowledge of tools, gear, and resources, and help fellow one-man bands and creative entrepreneurs figure things out together. PodcasterToolkit.co was the site that existed in idea-form from the very beginning — a well-organized home for carefully curated tools and resources, built as a one-stop shop for creators to get things done. No fluff, just the good stuff.

## The Big Picture Plan

**The blog** will be loaded with practical, instructional how-tos about podcasting and related topics — repurposing podcast scripts into blog entries along the way. **The shop** will feature podcasting and content-creation gear, linked directly to Amazon. **The tools section** will cover business and productivity software actually used and loved, like [FreshBooks](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/freshbooks), [Scrivener](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/scrivener), [Elegant Themes](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/divi), and Lynda.com. **Tutorials & videos** will build out a YouTube library over time.

## Steps to Launch

- **Planning:** define the purpose of the site and what it should actually do.
- **Web hosting:** [Bluehost](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/bluehost) — an existing account allowed unlimited sites, making this easy.
- **Theme:** the Extra theme, a magazine-style sibling to Divi by Elegant Themes, well-suited to content-heavy sites like this one.
- **Domain name:** [GoDaddy](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/godaddy). Multiple spelling variations (podcastertoolkit.co and podcasterstoolkit.co) were secured in case of typos, and unused domains get forwarded to active sites so they still serve some purpose.
- **Folder structure:** one central place created for theme files and assets, even before those assets existed.
- **Big tip:** stop overthinking and start — take baby steps, get a simple plan moving, and don't let endless planning replace actually beginning.
- **Branding & logos:** kept simple and quick — a type-based logo is perfectly fine.
- **Photo management:** Adobe Lightroom, with heavy use of Collections. Gear and tools get cataloged in the [Home Inventory app](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/home-inventory/id413564952?mt=12) from the App Store.

## Money

**Affiliate marketing** (see [Episode 44](/podcast/ep44/)) is the main monetization plan, through partnerships with [Amazon](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/amazon), FreshBooks, Elegant Themes, and Bluehost. **On content:** don't wait for everything to be finished — hit publish and build it out as you go.

## Takeaways

Do what makes you happy — this new site genuinely does. And when starting something new, the actual first step is simply to start.
