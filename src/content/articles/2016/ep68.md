## What Is Adobe After Effects?

[After Effects](https://goo.gl/4426N5) is a motion graphics and special effects tool. In Adobe's words: "the industry-standard animation and creative compositing app lets you design and deliver professional motion graphics and visual effects for film, TV, video, and web."

It shares real similarities with Photoshop — both work in layers with effects applied on top — but where Photoshop handles photos and still artwork, After Effects is built around motion. It's fundamentally a compositing tool, capable of complex motion graphics and effects, and it's where green-screen work (chroma keying) and camera tracking really shine — and where things get genuinely complicated. Any time a movie, TV show, or even a local news broadcast shows a slick effect, there's a good chance After Effects is behind it.

**Typical pro workflow:** Adobe Premiere Pro for assembling and editing, After Effects for special effects and polish, Adobe Audition for sound design.

## Workflow: Making the PodShed Holiday Gift Guide Videos

See [all the videos here](https://mikemurphy.co/podshed2016/).

1. **Adobe Draw** — hand-draw each product or tool using an iPad Pro and Apple Pencil.
2. **AirDrop** the final drawing over to the MacBook.
3. **Adobe After Effects** — create a new composition (the scene/canvas the animation gets built in) for each video, sized to 1920×1080p at 24fps.
4. **Photoshop or Illustrator** — build any additional graphics, backgrounds, or textures.
5. **Adobe Character Animator** — animate the "Mini-Mike" character.
6. **Audio/soundtrack** — [AudioBlocks](http://AudioBlocks.com) ($99/year for unlimited tracks, personal or commercial use). **Side tip:** if a YouTube copyright claim comes in on audio that's genuinely licensed, email the source — they can clear the claim and get the warning removed quickly.
7. **Story** — build a 60-second-or-shorter narrative around the product or tool.
8. **Adobe Media Encoder** — export as HD 1920×1080p `.mp4`.
9. **Upload** to Instagram, share to Facebook and Twitter.
10. **Upload to YouTube**, then embed that link in the corresponding post on PodcasterToolkit.co and MikeMurphy.co.
