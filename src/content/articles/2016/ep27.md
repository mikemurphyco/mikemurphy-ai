## Ask Mike Anything: Turning Blab Interviews into a Podcast

**Question:** How do you turn Blab interviews into a podcast on iTunes? I just don't get it.

**Answer:** Blab emails an audio and video file after each completed session. Take the video file into Audition (or any DAW), edit the audio just like a normal recorded episode, export as an MP3, and upload it to a podcast host the same way as any other audio file.

## Podfest Tampa 2016

**What:** Podfest Tampa 2016, February 25-26. **Organizers:** Chris & Katie Krimitsos.

**Highlight 1:** the most organized event attended to date.

**Highlight 2:** single-track learning kept sessions focused and easy to follow.

**Highlight 3: networking.** Meeting new people across the industry.

**Highlight 4: learning.** Genuinely inspiring guest speakers with fresh insights and perspectives. **Speakers mentioned:** [Jeff Brown of Read to Lead Podcast](http://readtoleadpodcast.com), [P. Desmond Adams](http://pdesmondadams.com) (Twitter presentation), and [Danny Pena of Gamertag Radio](http://www.gamertagradio.com).

**Highlight 5:** vendors, gear, and microphone testing on the show floor. **Volunteer mentioned:** [Marc Johansen](http://motoanswers.com).

**Highlight 6:** self-reflection and personal growth.

## Conclusion

Was it worth the price of admission? Absolutely. Glad it happened? No question. Back for Podfest 2017? Most likely. Recommended for others? 100%. Chris & Katie Krimitsos deserve a genuine thank you and shout-out for the effort — great job.
