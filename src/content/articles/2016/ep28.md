- A full gear cost breakdown: mics, mixers, interfaces, headphones, cables, and more
- The software and web services needed to actually run a podcast
- Recurring monthly costs beyond one-time gear purchases
- Why scripting (in some form) beats free-form conversation almost every time

## Ask Mike Anything: Scripting Podcasts

**Question:** What's the take on scripting podcasts?

**Answer:** This show gets scripted in a conversational style. Preparation is the key to a good episode — whether that means a full script, an outline, or bullet points depends on personal style, but free-form conversation rarely produces a good result. Skipping preparation is, in most cases, disrespectful to the audience — listeners give up a real chunk of their day to tune in, and it's the host's job to deliver value rather than waste that time. **Bottom line:** prepare for every episode the way you'd prepare for a speech or presentation. Ever sat through a presentation where the speaker was clearly winging it? Painful, right?
