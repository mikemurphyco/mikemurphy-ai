**Episode Summary:** What Podcasts Connect is and how to use it, plus the results of taking the StrengthsFinder 2.0 test — and why understanding your strengths (and weaknesses) matters for podcasting, business, and life.

## What You'll Learn

- What Podcasts Connect is and how to submit a podcast to iTunes through it
- What the StrengthsFinder 2.0 test measures and what it costs
- The 5 results from Mike's own test, broken down one by one
- Why what you do with the results matters more than the test itself

## What Is Podcasts Connect?

[PodcastsConnect.com](http://podcastsconnect.com) is Apple's portal for podcasters to manage all of their shows — also the portal for submitting new podcasts to iTunes, replacing the old in-iTunes submission method. **To access:** log in with the Apple ID used to create the podcast. **Warning:** don't mess with the feed or mirror feed settings unless you really know what you're doing.

## StrengthsFinder 2.0

Special thanks to [Jim Collison of The Average Guy TV](https://theaverageguy.tv/) network for the nudge to take [StrengthsFinder 2.0](https://www.gallupstrengthscenter.com/). The test costs $15, runs 177 questions, and takes under 30 minutes — the output is 5 strengths out of a possible 34, plus a full analysis and report. The test itself is the easy part; what actually gets done with the results is what matters.

## My Results

Top 5 strengths, in order:

1. **Strategic** — able to sort through clutter and find the best route forward. Not a skill that can be taught; it's a perspective that sees patterns where others see only complexity.
2. **Achiever** — a constant need for achievement. Every day starts at zero, and something tangible has to get done by the end of it to feel good — workdays, weekends, vacations, no exceptions.
3. **Responsibility** — psychological ownership over anything committed to, large or small, followed through to completion. A good name depends on it — if delivery isn't possible, an automatic search begins for ways to make it up to the other person. Apologies alone aren't enough.
4. **Learner** — drawn to the process of learning itself, more than the content or result. Energized by the steady, deliberate journey from ignorance to competence, regardless of subject.
5. **Relator** — pulled toward people already known rather than constantly meeting new ones. Deep satisfaction comes from working hard alongside close friends toward a shared goal.
