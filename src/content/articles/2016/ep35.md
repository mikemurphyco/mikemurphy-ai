## Why Snapchat Is Being Used Here

**Update:** 10 billion videos are viewed per day on Snapchat. The platform was still young at the time, and the marketing potential — promoting a personal brand by teaching and sharing behind-the-scenes footage of the workflow and process — was clear early on.

**People mentioned:** [Gary Vaynerchuk](https://www.garyvaynerchuk.com), [John Lee Dumas](http://www.eofire.com/), and [Cliff Ravenscraft](http://podcastanswerman.com/).

## 5 Strategies for Using Snapchat

1. **[Ghost Codes](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/ghostcodes-discovery-app-for/id1083057569?mt=8)** — a directory of Snapchat users searchable by interest tag.

   ![ghostcodes](/assets/media/2016/05/ghostcodes-150x150.jpg)

2. **Teaching.** Share what's known, build authority, give tutorials or teach a niche topic directly on Snapchat.
3. **Repurpose content.** Cross-post Snaps to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, and Pinterest.
4. **Audience engagement.** Give the audience direct access to you and your story.
5. **Behind the scenes.** Show off the workspace and how the work actually gets done.

## Benefits of Using Snapchat (So Far)

Genuinely enjoyable, increased confidence on camera, improved teaching style, a growing network of new connections, new podcast listeners, establishing authority, a boost in creativity, and better productivity/accountability.
