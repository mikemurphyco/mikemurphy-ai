**Episode Summary:** Part 3 of the self-publishing series — where to sell a book beyond Amazon, tools that make formatting painless, and done-for-you distribution services worth knowing about.

## What You'll Learn

- eBook marketplaces beyond Amazon, and what makes each one different
- The most common eBook formats and why formatting trips up so many self-publishers
- Pressbooks and Vellum as time-saving formatting tools, with real pricing
- Done-for-you distribution services and the tradeoff they involve

**Recap — [Episode 72 (Part 1)](/podcast/ep72/):** writing and editing tools — [Scrivener](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/scrivener), [Ulysses](https://www.ulyssesapp.com/), [Bear](https://bear.app), [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/), [Hemingway App](https://www.hemingwayapp.com/), plus [750words.com](http://750words.com) as an accountability tool.

**Recap — [Episode 73 (Part 2)](/podcast/ep73/):** self-publishing on Amazon.com via [Kindle Direct Publishing](https://kdp.amazon.com/) for the eBook, [CreateSpace](https://www.createspace.com/) for on-demand print copies, and [ACX](https://www.acx.com) for converting a book into an audiobook — plus a look at money-making opportunities for podcasters and content creators to narrate audiobooks.

## Other eBook Marketplaces (Amazon Competitors)

- **[Apple iBooks Store](https://www.apple.com/ibooks/)** — built for iPhone and iPad; no browser-accessible storefront.
- **[Kobo Writing Life](https://www.kobo.com/writinglife)** — a popular self-publishing platform with a 5-step process that distributes globally; payout kicks in once royalties exceed $50. Kobo's e-reader device competes directly with Kindle and tends to be more popular internationally.
- **[Barnes & Noble](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/b/nook-books/_/N-8qa)** — the Nook lives on, though Amazon continues to squeeze B&N's market share.
- **[Blurb](https://www.blurb.com/)** — a genuine favorite and a self-publishing pioneer, making it possible to publish without upfront costs. Readable on Kindle, iOS, and Android.
- **[Google Play Books](https://play.google.com/Books)** — the Android book platform; criticized for a clunky upload process, but Google's search power makes it worthwhile.
- **Bonus: [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/)** — books, audiobooks, and more in one subscription.

## Formatting Made Easy

Common eBook formats — PDF, ePub, AZW, MOBI — are where a lot of self-publishers get overwhelmed. [Scrivener](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/scrivener), [Ulysses](https://www.ulyssesapp.com/), and [Adobe InDesign](http://indesign.adobe.com) can all handle every format, but getting the design to hold up cleanly across Kindle, Nook, Kobo, and iPad takes real work.

**Two time-saving formatting tools:** [Pressbooks](http://Pressbooks.com) and [Vellum](http://Vellum.pub). Note: the book still has to be written elsewhere, and it still has to be manually uploaded to each selling platform — but these tools handle only the formatting, with no ongoing royalty cut.

**Pressbooks** — a serious contender worth considering, and a genuine timesaver. Costs range from $19.99–$99 per book; accounts still need to be created and uploaded to Kindle, iBooks, Google, etc., but Pressbooks handles the heavy formatting work and packages it cleanly for multi-platform distribution.

**Vellum** — not quite as feature-rich as Pressbooks, but built to "easily create beautiful eBooks." $19.99 per book à la carte, or $199 for unlimited books. Upload a manuscript, and Vellum outputs every format needed for the major eBook sellers.

## Distribution Made Easy

Amazon is step one for most self-publishers, but several popular done-for-you platforms have partnerships with every major eBook seller worldwide — upload once, and they handle distribution everywhere else. Convenient and a genuine time-saver, but the per-book payout is lower than doing it manually.
