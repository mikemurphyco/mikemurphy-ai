## The Steps (Part 1): Planning & Pre-Launch

**Step 1 — Start.** Commit to starting a podcast and don't overthink it.

**Step 2 — Time.** Is there genuinely room in the work/life schedule to produce a podcast consistently? If yes, move forward.

**Step 3 — Why?** Get honest about the real reason and goal behind starting.

**Step 4 — Topic & audience.** Know the topic, and visualize the target audience clearly.

**Step 5 — Format.** Solo show or interview-style? How long will episodes run?

**Step 6 — Title.** What's the show called?

**Step 7 — Domain.** Buy the domain name and start thinking through a website to house the show.

**Step 8 — Artwork & branding.** Cover art, headshots, promo photos, and a general visual identity are all needed.

**Step 9 — Gear.** Buy the equipment: dynamic microphone, audio interface/mixer, swivel boom arm, headphones.

**Step 10 — DAW.** Decide on a digital audio workstation for recording and editing (Adobe Audition, GarageBand, Audacity, etc.).
