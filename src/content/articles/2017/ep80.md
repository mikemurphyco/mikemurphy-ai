**Episode Summary:** A behind-the-scenes walkthrough of how show notes actually get made each week — writing, formatting, episode artwork, and the full publishing workflow on WordPress.

## What You'll Learn

- What show notes are and why they matter beyond just being a text afterthought
- Mike's 8-step weekly workflow, from artwork to newsletter to social sharing
- The core rule for not hating show notes: build a repeatable process
- The full toolchain: InDesign, Scrivener, Byword, WordPress, Libsyn, PowerPress, Mailchimp, Buffer

**What are show notes?** The blog post that accompanies every episode — where links and resources mentioned in the episode live, along with an overview of what listeners can expect.

## Show Notes: How to Not Hate Them

1. Build a repeatable process.
2. Write the script or outline with show notes already in mind — don't treat them as an afterthought.
3. Find balance, and don't agonize over minutiae.

## The Weekly Show Notes Workflow

**Step 1 — episode artwork.** Built in Adobe InDesign, roughly 5–10 minutes.

**Step 2 — craft the show notes outline.** Duplicate the script and copy the show notes content to the clipboard, using Scrivener. Roughly 10–20 minutes.

**Step 3 — multi-markdown formatting.** Paste the script into Byword and use multi-markdown (HTML shortcodes) to format it for the WordPress post.

**Step 4 — create the blog post.** New post in WordPress.org using the Divi theme. Tip: append `/wp-admin` to the domain to log in. Schedule the post for Friday publishing, update the permalink for easy sharing, and swap in the episode artwork and featured image.

**Step 5 — grab the audio link.** Log into Libsyn, copy the MP3 link and the podcast player embed code.

**Step 5.5 — wire it into the post.** Paste the MP3 URL into the PowerPress plugin by Blubrry.

**Step 6 — prep the newsletter.** Build it out in MailChimp.

**Step 7 — make a promo video** for the episode.

**Step 8 — share it.** Use Buffer to send the show notes page out to Facebook, Twitter, and Google+. Rinse and repeat.
