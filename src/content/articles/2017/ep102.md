**Episode Summary:** A full walkthrough of Mike's Instagram and Instagram Stories workflow and strategy — why daily content, the exact production steps, and the case for why more creators and businesses should be using it.

## What You'll Learn

- Instagram usage and business-adoption stats as of 2017
- Mike's exact 6-step daily video workflow, from topic to AirDrop upload
- Why daily video-making started as a way to discover strengths and weaknesses
- The full morning routine and Instagram Stories rhythm behind the consistency

Follow along: [@mikeunplugged](http://instagram.com/mikeunplugged) on Instagram.

## Instagram Facts & Figures (2017)

<!-- REVIEW: Instagram usage stats and revenue figures below are from 2017 and are now significantly outdated -->

Source: [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-stats/). In 2016, Instagram had 300 million active users; by January 2017 ([Episode 71: The Instagram Live Show](/podcast/ep71/)), that had grown to 600 million; by this episode, over 700 million. On the business side, 70% of US businesses were using Instagram (8 million business profiles), up from 48% (1.5 million profiles) in 2016. Mobile ad revenue hit $4 billion in 2017, with 25% of all ads being video. Instagram Stories had 250 million active daily users. On Facebook, 85% of ads were watched without sound — a strong argument for captions and text overlays. Favorite stat: roughly 50% of Instagram text contains emojis.

**Tip #1:** mobile content matters — audit your website and always be thinking about what people will actually consume on a phone.

## Why Instagram Is the Favorite Channel

Design, photography, video, and creative people cluster there, and visual creativity is genuinely inspiring to be surrounded by. As of this episode: at least one piece of hand-made content uploaded to Instagram for 693 days straight, including 290 consecutive days of one video per day since November 1, 2016 — and essentially no missed days on Instagram Stories since its release.

## Why Make Daily Videos?

It started as a way to discover strengths and weaknesses and get genuinely good at making videos, tutorials, and teaching. Building a real personal brand and business takes skill and consistent effort — daily practice is simply the fastest way to get there, and the learning never really stops.

## Daily Workflow & Routine

Each video typically takes 1–3 hours.

1. **Topic** — something tied to content creation, personal branding, podcasting, or a tool that's genuinely useful.
2. **Record & edit** — [Camtasia](http://mikemurphy.co/camtasia) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass --> and [ScreenFlow](http://mikemurphy.co/screenflow) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass --> for screen recording (computer and iOS), plus a [Logitech C920](http://mikemurphy.co/c920) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass --> for webcam footage and simple b-roll.
3. **Adobe Draw** — hand-drawn icons or logos when needed, using an iPad Pro and Apple Pencil.
4. **Thumbnails** — created in Photoshop, both square and horizontal formats.
5. **Write the description** — a step-by-step writeup in Apple Notes (syncs instantly across devices). **Tip:** always write real descriptions with hashtags, keywords, and emojis for SEO.
6. **AirDrop to iPhone** — for the final upload to Instagram.

## Morning Routine

Up at 4am: news, updates, emails, a YouTube video or two, sports scores — personal time before the workday starts. Uploads go out to YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, and sometimes Medium in the morning, with the Instagram post going up last.

## Instagram Stories Rhythm

After morning posting and admin work: a walk, exercise, and a good-morning Story. Updates continue throughout the day, usually wrapping with sunset beach time. **Motivation tip:** if being on camera feels intimidating, Instagram Stories and Live are great low-pressure warmups — the content disappears, so there's nothing permanent to worry about.

## Instagram Live

Something of a dud so far, but not abandoned — a new collaboration feature was on the way and looked promising enough to justify teaming up with others and giving Live another real push.

## Networking

Engage with people in your space or people you want to know better — like, comment, and don't be afraid to send a direct message.

## Final Thoughts

Instagram has real momentum, backed by Facebook's scale, and it's a strong recommendation for any content creator, brand, or business willing to give it a genuine try.
