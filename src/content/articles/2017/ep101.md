**Episode Summary:** Tips and strategies for building your own public relations department — how to prepare for podcast interviews and livestream appearances, craft your story, build a media kit, and actually deliver value as a guest.

## What You'll Learn

- Why being your own PR firm means treating every post, tweet, and comment as brand representation
- How to prepare to be a great podcast or livestream guest: story, media kit, and delivering value
- What to include in a personal media kit, and why a Pretty Link makes it easy to share
- Why consistent story and language across appearances helps people remember you

**Inspiration for this episode:** a growing number of inquiries to collaborate with other businesses and appear as a guest on podcasts and livestreams.

## Mike Murphy, LLC

The business is centered entirely on the personal brand — there's genuinely no separation. Every Facebook comment, tweet, Instagram post, podcast episode, and YouTube tutorial, personal or business-related, represents the brand. In practice, that means being your own public relations firm.

**What PR actually means:** spreading information to the public, listeners, and followers — for free. The goal is raising awareness and building a positive image so people get to know and like you or your business. PR isn't direct marketing or selling; it's about painting the picture and telling the story in a way that earns the attention of the audience worth serving.

**Mike Murphy LLC's "PR firm" in practice:** a weekly podcast, daily tutorials, articles on Medium, social media engagement, and daily Instagram Stories content.

## PR Strategies

Being a guest on other podcasts and livestreams — a direct way to gain exposure in front of an audience that doesn't yet know you.

## Preparing to Be a Guest

**Craft and practice your story.** Use the same words and framing across every appearance, tailoring only slightly as needed — consistency is what makes people remember you. **Tip:** an assessment like StrengthsFinder 2.0 can surface language worth building directly into your own branding, since the results tend to describe exactly who you are.

**Create a media kit.** Think of it as a cheat sheet for the host — everything they need to introduce you properly and build a blog post or social posts around the appearance.

## What Goes in a Media Kit

- A 2-page Google Doc PDF (who you are, what you do, tagline, website and social URLs with handles)
- 3 images (square and wide headshots, plus podcast cover art)

**Tip:** host everything in Dropbox or on your WordPress site, and create a [Pretty Link](https://wordpress.org/plugins/pretty-link/) — a short, memorable URL for sharing (e.g. `mikemurphy.co/headshots`).

## Delivering Value

A host invited you on their show to make them and their audience look good — deliver real value, whatever your specialty is. Double down here, and the payoff is real: new listeners, the host's genuine respect, a future invite, and being top of mind whenever someone asks that host for a referral.
