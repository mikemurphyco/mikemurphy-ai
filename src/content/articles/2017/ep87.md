## The 10-Step Client Workflow

**Step 1 — gather contact information.** Everything goes into Gmail contacts, with as many notes as possible since notes are searchable and help recall client details later. Tips: pull contact info from email signatures, check a client's website contact page for social channels and business address, keep a complete signature on outgoing email, and use intake forms ([Google Forms](https://www.google.com/forms/about/) or [Wufoo](http://wufoo.com)) to gather everything needed to start a project, logos and assets included.

**Step 2 — Gmail labels.** Every new client email gets a label nested under "Clients."

**Step 3 — Google Drive.** Similar to Dropbox — each client gets their own folder.

**Step 4 — Google Calendar.** Every appointment, meeting, deadline, commitment, trip, and important date goes on the calendar, with reminders set for 1 day and 30 minutes before.

**Step 5 — Apple Reminders.** The instant something goes on the calendar or comes to mind, Siri gets asked to set a reminder.

**Step 6 — Calendly.** Sent to clients as a scheduling link — they pick from open time slots, and [Calendly](https://calendly.com/) syncs with Google Calendar to avoid double-booking automatically.

**Step 7 — FreshBooks.** Time tracking, estimates, invoicing, accounting, and expense tracking — $25/month, and genuinely recommended.

**Step 8 — Google Docs.** One running history doc per client, logging everything done for each session or job. [Dropbox Paper](https://www.dropbox.com/paper) is a solid alternative for non-Gmail users.

**Step 9 — 1Password.** Client passwords and sensitive data get stored in secure notes.

**Step 10 — collect payment in FreshBooks.** Send a thank-you message, and keep the momentum going toward the next client and project.
