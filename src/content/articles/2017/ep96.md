**Episode Summary:** A mid-year, behind-the-scenes look at the 5 ways video powers Mike Murphy LLC today, and the strategy and focus for video through the second half of 2017.

## What You'll Learn

- The 5 categories video serves for Mike Murphy LLC: branding, promotion, education, marketing, revenue
- What's currently being made in each category, and the mobile gear used to make it
- The moving-forward plan for each category through the rest of 2017
- Why podcast promo teasers get repurposed across every channel, not just posted once

Technology and trends move fast, so this episode is a mid-year check-in on the video strategy — shared to help you better promote your own podcast, personal brand, and business.

## The 5 Main Ways Video Gets Used

Personal branding, podcast promotion, education, advertising & marketing, and revenue generation.

### Personal Branding

The core of the business — building around a name and a person. Podcasting/audio and video remain the two most intimate, effective ways to promote a personal brand.

**Currently making:** [Instagram Stories](http://instagram.com/mikeunplugged), Facebook & Instagram Live, and daily quick tips on YouTube.

**Mobile tools used:** [DJI Osmo](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/osmomobile), [Manfrotto Pixi Tripod](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/pixi), [MeFoto Sidekick 360](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/sidekick), [Rode VideoMicro](http://mikemurphy.co/rodemicro) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->, [Manfrotto Lumimuse LED light](http://mikemurphy.co/lumimuse) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->.

**Moving forward:** more documentary and vlogging-style content.

### Podcast Promotion

**Currently making:** a weekly teaser video built in [Adobe After Effects](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/adobe) for each episode, shared across the website, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and Medium. Side tip: always link back to the actual episode.

**Moving forward:** break each episode into bite-sized clips instead of one single teaser, to generate more interest per episode.

### Education

Tutorial videos that go deeper than the daily quick tips.

**Currently making:** daily quick tips, plus product demos and tutorials.

**Moving forward:** more gear and product demos aimed specifically at podcasters and content creators.

### Advertising & Marketing

Direct marketing through video ads.

**Currently making:** boosted Facebook and Instagram ads and promos, aimed at reaching a larger audience.

**Moving forward:** more promo videos advertising the podcast, website, and channels to build broader buzz.

### Revenue Generation

**Currently making:** a couple of animated tool videos for local businesses, plus promo videos for [t-shirt designs](http://mikemurphy.co/shirts) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->.

**Moving forward:** more products aimed at podcasters and creators, each promoted with its own branded video.
