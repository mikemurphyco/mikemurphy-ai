## 10 Principles Behind the Mike Murphy LLC Brand

- **Travel is a priority.** The ultimate experience and education life has to offer — freedom to explore and travel is the entire reason for being in business independently.
- **Self-sufficiency.** A one-man band unafraid to try new things, trusting the ability to handle whatever comes up.
- **Adaptability.** Nimble and quick to pivot — a genuine strength, and one that helps others navigate change too.
- **Less is more.** The simple things are really all that's needed or wanted.
- **Community & relationships.** Connection and kindness guide how business gets done — treating people with common courtesy and respect is always the right call.
- **Creativity.** Never stop making things, and keep pushing personal limits.
- **Uniqueness.** Drive your own ship. No permission needed to do your own thing.
- **Confidence.** Trust the gut and the intuition — giving it everything is what leads to success.
- **Sharing.** Give value to others first; the money and opportunities follow from that, not the other way around.
- **Life is good.** Don't forget to smile once in a while and enjoy the ride.
