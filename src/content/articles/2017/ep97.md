**Episode Summary:** A behind-the-scenes look at making the "One Man Band: Create the Life You Want" promo video — the idea behind it, the 2-day shoot, and whether it hit the mark.

## What You'll Learn

- What "One Man Band" means as a personal brand identity
- The goal behind the One Man Band: Create the Life You Want promo video
- A 2-day shoot breakdown: sunrise setup, sunset drone and Glidecam work, plus b-roll days
- The post-production stack used to finish it: Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Artlist.io

## What Does "One Man Band" Mean?

Someone multifaceted, unafraid to carve their own path — self-motivated, resourceful, and not reliant on anyone else to get things done. A person on a mission, achieving what they want without waiting on others.

## The Goal of the Video

A promotional video for Mike Murphy LLC, aimed at potential clients and brands, and a proper introduction for anyone visiting the website or discovering the content for the first time. The intent: come across as a podcaster and tech guy, creative and a little different, hardworking, with a genuinely positive message. Simple in concept, harder in execution.

## How the Video Was Made

**Day 1 (sunrise):** the One Man Band microphone stand, a Canon T5i DSLR, a GoPro Hero 4, and an iPhone 6 all set up together, with [Andrew Budz](http://instagram.com/mclaughlz) operating the drone.

**Day 2 (sunset):** a bike ride shot with a [DJI Osmo](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/osmomobile) and iPhone 6, with Andrew on a Vespa running a Glidecam and drone simultaneously — an ambitious, slightly chaotic setup.

**Multiple days:** additional b-roll shooting.

**Post-production:** Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects, with music from [Artlist.io](http://artlist.io).

## Was It Worth It?

Yes. As a creative, there's always some self-doubt when the finished product doesn't perfectly match what was pictured going in — but the final video landed well, mostly because it genuinely conveyed the tone and inspiration it set out to deliver.
