**Episode Summary:** A behind-the-scenes look at how Mike's daily Quick Tip tutorial videos come together — topic sourcing, screencasting software, export sizing for every platform, and the full sharing workflow.

## What You'll Learn

- Where daily quick-tip topic ideas actually come from, whiteboard included
- The exact screencasting tools used to record and edit each video
- Every export size needed across YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and Twitter
- The full folder structure and sharing workflow, from AirDrop to Medium

**What are Quick Tip videos?** Daily tip videos about podcasting and content creation, [posted to Instagram](http://instagram.com/mikeunplugged) and [YouTube](http://mikemurphy.co/youtube) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->.

## Step 1: Pick a Topic

A running list of tutorial ideas lives on a whiteboard, plus in [Trello](http://trello.com) and [Workflowy](http://workflowy.com). Need more? Check email and DMs for real questions people are already asking, browse social media discussions, or dig into [Twitter Advanced Search](https://twitter.com/search-advanced?lang=en), [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/), and [Quora](http://quora.com).

## Step 2: Recording & Editing

Screencasting software records the computer screen, iOS devices, or webcam. **Recording tip:** shoot at full resolution — a 2880×1800 Retina display gives plenty of room to scale down to 1920×1080 without any quality loss.

**Tools used to record:** [Camtasia 3/9](http://mikemurphy.co/camtasia) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->, [ScreenFlow](http://mikemurphy.co/screenflow) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->, and QuickTime (free on Mac). **Tools used to edit:** Camtasia.

## Step 3: Exporting & Formatting

**Terminology worth knowing:** P = pixels, HD = High Definition, frame rate (30fps or 24fps), 16:9 = wide aspect ratio, 1:1 = square aspect ratio, MP4 = video file extension, H.264 = the standard web video codec.

**Recommended export sizes:**

- **YouTube:** 1920×1080p
- **Website:** 1920×1080p
- **Medium:** 1920×1080p
- **Facebook:** 1920×1080 and 1080×1080p
- **Twitter:** 1920×1080 and 1080×1080p
- **Instagram posts:** 1080×1080 (square), 864×1080 (vertical), 1800×945 (horizontal), 1920×1080 (wide)
- **Instagram Stories:** 1080×1920 (vertical)

## Step 4: Sharing Online

**Folder structure:** Assets (graphics/photos), Thumbnails (YouTube covers), Audio (music), Project Files (Camtasia/ScreenFlow), Renders (final videos).

**The routine:** AirDrop the square video to iPhone, write the outline in the Notes app (synced via text), upload the square (1080×1080) version to [Instagram](http://instagram.com/mikeunplugged) with a description and the hashtag shortcut, then share to Twitter and Facebook directly from Instagram. Upload the wide (1920×1080) version to [YouTube](http://mikemurphy.co/youtube) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass --> with a description, and copy the embed link into a [Medium post](https://goo.gl/akRBFy).

## Cliff Notes Version

Pick a topic. Record screen, iOS device, or webcam. Edit in Camtasia. Export as a 1080×1080 square video for Instagram, Facebook, and Twitter, and a duplicate at 1920×1080. AirDrop the square version to iPhone. Upload to [Instagram](http://instagram.com/mikeunplugged), share to [Twitter](http://twitter.com/mikeunplugged) and Facebook. Upload the wide version to [YouTube](http://mikemurphy.co/youtube) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->, copy the embed link, and write it up on [Medium](https://goo.gl/akRBFy).
