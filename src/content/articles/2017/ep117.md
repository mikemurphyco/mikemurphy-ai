**Episode Summary:** A full blueprint of how Mike launched his side hustle, Murphy Concierge — a local tech-handyman service — covering the why, the exact tools and steps used to get it off the ground, and the revenue goals behind it.

## What You'll Learn

- What Murphy Concierge is and why Mike started it as a local tech-handyman side hustle
- The exact steps and tools used to launch the business: domain, website, logo, invoicing
- What a side hustle is, and two podcasts to learn more about building one
- Mike's revenue goal and the math behind his introductory hourly rate

**Who this episode is for:** anyone looking to add a side hustle alongside an existing business or full/part-time job.

**What is a side hustle?** Per [Entrepreneur Magazine](http://entrepreneur.com): *"A side hustle is a way to make some extra cash that allows you flexibility to pursue what you're most interested in. It can also be your true passion – a chance to delve into fashion, travel or whatever it is you care about the most without quitting your day job."*

**Podcasts about side hustles worth checking out:**

- [Side Hustle Nation](https://www.sidehustlenation.com/) by Nick Loper
- [Side Hustle School](https://sidehustleschool.com/) by Chris Guillebeau

## What Is Murphy Concierge?

A local side hustle helping people fix their iDevices and Macs, plus troubleshooting, fixing, and training for digital products and online tools. Tagline: **The Tech Handyman**. Site (in progress at the time): [murphyconcierge.com](https://Www.murphyconcierge.com), email: mike@murphyconcierge.com.

## Why Start This Side Hustle?

To help pay the bills while building the online personal brand and business — and because it's a service Mike already does and loves, just one he hadn't been marketing until now.

## Steps and Tools Used to Get Started

**Deciding to start:**

- Secure a domain name via [GoDaddy](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/godaddy)
- Check social handle availability with [Namecheckr](https://Namecheckr.com)

**Building the website:**

- Point the domain at [SiteGround](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/siteground) (the web host used at the time) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->
- Install WordPress.org
- Download and install the [Divi WordPress theme](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/divi)
- Set up [Google Workspace](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/gsuite) to send/receive mail at mike@murphyconcierge.com <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->
- Create a logo using [Adobe Illustrator](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/illustrator)

## Logo & Brand Resources

- [Canva](https://Canva.com) (free)
- [Logo.squarespace.com](https://Logo.squarespace.com) ($10)
- **Color palettes:** [Adobe Color](https://color.adobe.com/), [Coolors](https://Coolors.co)
- **Business cards:** [Adobe InDesign](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/indesign) or [Canva](https://Canva.com)
- **Digital business cards:** [Adobe After Effects](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/aftereffects), [Keynote](https://www.apple.com/keynote), [ScreenFlow](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/screenflow), or [Camtasia](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/camtasia)
- **Invoicing:** [FreshBooks](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/freshbooks) (main tool), [PayPal](http://paypal.com), [Square](http://squareup.com), [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) (for web)

## Goals for Murphy Concierge

Enjoy the work and generate $5,000/month. At an introductory rate of $75/hour, that works out to roughly 66 jobs a month, or about 2.2 per day.

**Marketing tool mentioned:** [NextDoor](https://nextdoor.com/) (iOS/Android)
