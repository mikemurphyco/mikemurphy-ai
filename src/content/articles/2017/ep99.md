**Episode Summary:** A behind-the-scenes look at the real financials of Mike Murphy Unplugged — what it cost to start the podcast, and what it costs to keep it running week after week.

## What You'll Learn

- The real dollar breakdown of Mike's full studio podcast setup: $942
- Monthly recurring software and hosting costs: $83/month
- A mobile recording kit built for $500, and a beginner studio kit for $486
- Mike's buying philosophy: simple, reputable, and reliable over expensive or feature-heavy

## Buying Philosophy

The goal is always the best quality affordable, not the most expensive thing possible. Gear with a good reputation, a simple design, and a track record of just working — not bells and whistles — wins every time.
