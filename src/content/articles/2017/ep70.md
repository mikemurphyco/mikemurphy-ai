**Episode Summary:** The first-ever annual roadmap episode — Mike's 2017 theme of simplicity, the full list of business priorities, and a concrete content schedule for the year ahead.

## What You'll Learn

- The 2017 theme: simplicity and minimalism, physically and mentally
- The full list of Mike Murphy LLC business priorities for the year
- A concrete weekly/monthly content schedule across podcast, video, writing, and social
- Why the annual roadmap episode became a recurring tradition (later referenced in ep100, ep121, and ep122)

*"If you don't know where you are going, any road will get you there."*

## Setting the Tone for 2017

Simplicity — a personal version of minimalism. Clutter-free physical and mental environments: selling off unused stuff, giving away unworn clothes, cleaning out junk drawers, organizing and backing up hard drives. **Tip:** Facebook Marketplace is a great outlet for selling things.

**Santosha** — contentment and satisfaction, or more precisely, being totally satisfied desiring nothing beyond the fundamentals, doing one's best, and accepting the results of that effort.

## Business Priorities for 2017

**Generating income & marketing services:** brainstorm sessions for creative entrepreneurs and small brands, WordPress caretaker and concierge work, Photoshop concierge services, website installs, and Google Apps for Work installs.

**PodcasterToolkit.co:** add content and continue building out this side project.

**Tutorials & classes:** more videos and classes for YouTube, Skillshare, and personal sales.

**Video:** stays a big priority — livestreaming and video creation continue to increase, video editing skills and workflow get sharpened, a simple promo video introduces the business, and more off-the-cuff, motivational-style videos get made.

**Social media priorities:** [Instagram](http://instagram.com/mikeunplugged), Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest — livestreaming on Instagram and Facebook stays consistent and frequent.

**Writing:** continued regular Medium posting, plus LinkedIn and PodcasterToolkit.

**Book:** written in 2017 (a goal that, per later episodes, didn't fully materialize on schedule).

## Content Schedule for 2017

- **Podcast & newsletter:** 1 per week
- **Tutorials & YouTube:** up to 2 per week
- **Blog, LinkedIn & Medium.com articles:** up to 2 per week
- **Social media (Instagram):** 7 days per week
- **Live video (Instagram or Facebook):** 3 times per week
- **Skillshare classes:** 3 total
- **Personal class to market and sell:** 1
- **Book:** 1
