**Episode Summary:** Part 1 of 2 on attending Podcast Movement, the biggest conference in podcasting — pre-conference goals, expectations, budget, and the game plan for getting the most out of it.

## What You'll Learn

- What Podcast Movement is and the real cost of attending (roughly $2,000 all-in)
- The 5 goals driving Mike's first Podcast Movement: networking, clarity, education, inspiration, and fun
- A concrete networking strategy for conferences, business cards and elevator pitch included
- Why "watch, observe, ask questions" is the strategy for gaining business clarity at an event

This episode covers the pre-conference goals, expectations, and plan of attack for [Podcast Movement](http://podcastmovement.com), held in Anaheim, California, August 22–25, 2017. [Episode 104](/podcast/ep104/) picks up afterward with the actual experience and takeaways.

## What Is Podcast Movement?

A 4-year-old conference that started as a Kickstarter campaign, held this year in Anaheim from Tuesday, August 22 through Friday, August 25, 2017.

## The Investment

- Conference ticket: $250
- Hotel: $189/night × 4 nights
- Airfare: $400
- Per diem: $100
- **Total: roughly $2,000**

## Goals & Mindset

Five things going into the trip: networking, clarity, knowledge and education, inspiration and creativity, and travel/fun/social interaction.

**Networking.** By definition, interacting with others to exchange information and build contacts, especially to further a career or business. The plan: meet vendors, sponsors, podcasters, online friends, and listeners face to face — armed with uniquely designed business cards and a practiced elevator pitch (who am I, what do I do, who do I help). Listening matters as much as talking, so a set of genuine questions was prepared in advance to get people to open up.

**Clarity.** Looking for direction on the business by watching, observing, listening, and asking a lot of questions — what are other people doing right that could translate here?

**Knowledge & education.** A genuine tech nerd's approach: attend a mix of sessions, workshops, and vendor conversations to pick up new tools, gear, and techniques.

**Inspiration & creativity.** Attending to recharge and refresh the creative tank — the strategy is simple: seek out people with similar skills and interests, and repeat.

**Travel/fun/social interaction.** A genuine love of travel and exploring, plus the appeal of spending time around a crowd of fellow podcasters and creative people.

## Final Thoughts

Heading to Podcast Movement 2017 to learn, create, and move forward. Wish me luck.
