**Verdict up front:** an incredible time, genuinely great people, worth every penny, and a lock to return for Podcast Movement 2018.

## The Breakdown: Podcast Movement 2017

The 4th annual [Podcast Movement](http://podcastmovement.com), a conference for podcasters by podcasters, held August 22–25, 2017 at the Marriott convention center in Anaheim, California. Pre-conference goals and prep are covered in [Episode 103](/podcast/ep103/).

## Highlights

**People.** The top priority was meeting new people and strengthening relationships with familiar faces and online friends met in person for the first time. Conferences aren't easy — a genuine pit in the stomach walking into the Marriott — but plenty of fellow podcasters share the same introvert tendencies, which made it easier to push through. The New Attendee Orientation on day one was a helpful icebreaker.

**Keynotes.** [Dan Carlin of Hardcore History](http://www.dancarlin.com/hardcore-history-series/) and Aaron Mahnke of the [Lore Podcast](http://www.lorepodcast.com/) delivered genuinely impactful, inspiring talks. Their shared message: the playing field is level, and opportunity is abundant for anyone willing to be consistent with quality content and put in the work.

**Exhibition floor.** Adobe, Shure, and other major gear and software vendors, all in one place.

## Goals Met?

Yes — checked against all 5 goals from [Episode 103](/podcast/ep103/):

- **Networking** — a stack of new business cards and some genuinely great new relationships. Self-graded a solid B to B+, which tracks — this was expected to be the biggest challenge going in.
- **Clarity** — came away understanding the need to package the work more clearly so people immediately understand what's on offer. The answers were already there; Podcast Movement just sharpened the insight.
- **Knowledge & education** — a 1:1 lesson on audio compression at the Adobe Audition booth was good enough to have stayed at all day.
- **Inspiration & creativity** — left feeling genuinely recharged and more motivated than ever to push the podcast and business forward.
- **Travel/fun/social interaction** — California, and a day walking around Disney's downtown district, delivered on this front easily.

## The Big Takeaway

The business model needs fixing, fast — and what's actually for sale needs to be communicated clearly and directly.

## How It Gets Implemented

**Action one:** rebuild the "Hire Mike" page on the website — cut anything no longer relevant, and add real examples of the videos and tutorials being sold to businesses and brands. **Simplified message:** tutorial and how-to videos for businesses and content creators, promo videos for social media, and 1:1 problem-solving sessions over Skype. **Action two:** create at least one digital course for sale.

## Podcast Movement 2018?

Without a doubt.
