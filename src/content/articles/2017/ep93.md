**Episode Summary:** A practical follow-up to the Pixels 101 episode — exactly how to size photos and videos for Instagram's main feed and Instagram Stories, file types, aspect ratios, and pixel dimensions included.

## What You'll Learn

- What file types and video lengths Instagram accepts, feed vs. Stories
- The exact pixel dimensions for every Instagram post format: square, horizontal, vertical, and Stories
- How Mike's own export settings differ from Instagram's minimums for extra quality headroom
- Why aspect ratio fundamentals from Episode 92 carry directly into Instagram sizing decisions

## The Two Sides of Instagram

**Instagram Feed** — where photos and videos get posted and browsed for accounts you follow. **Instagram Stories** — similar to Snapchat, with its own size requirements, built for ongoing 24-hour posts.

**Past episodes on Instagram:** [Episode 37: The Instagram Show](/podcast/ep37/) and [Episode 49: The Instagram Stories Show](/podcast/ep49/).

## What File Types Does Instagram Accept?

**Photos:** JPEG or PNG. Animated GIFs are not supported — [Boomerang from Instagram](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/boomerang-from-instagram/id1041596399?mt=8) is the closest alternative. **Videos:** Instagram accepts a range of formats, but H.264 codec with an `.mp4` extension is the safest bet.

## Video Length Limits

- **Main feed:** 60 seconds or less
- **Instagram Stories:** 10 seconds or less

## Aspect Ratios & Dimensions

Builds directly on [Episode 92: The Pixels 101 Show](/podcast/ep92/) — aspect ratio is the width-to-height relationship of a photo or video. The most popular aspect ratio for video is 16:9 (wide), typically 1920×1080px HD. Resolution (measured in PPI) applies to photos, usually 72–96 PPI; PPI isn't a concern for video.

## How to Size Photos and Videos for Instagram

**Profile image:** 1:1 (square). Instagram's recommended size is 160×160px, but any 1:1 image works — Mike uses 1024×1024px (overkill, but Instagram scales it down cleanly).

**Square posts:** 1:1. Instagram's spec is 1080×1080px at 72 DPI for photos, 1080×1080px H.264 `.mp4` for video. Mike's own settings: 2048×2048px at 96 PPI (photos), 1920×1920px H.264 (video).

**Horizontal, option 1 (16:9 wide):** Instagram's spec is 1920×1080px at 72 DPI (photos) or 1920×1080px / 1280×720px H.264 (video). Mike's settings: 2048×2048px at 96 PPI (photos), 1920×1920px H.264 (video).

**Horizontal, option 2 (1.91:1 wide):** Instagram/Facebook spec is 1200×630px or 1080×566px for photos and video. Mike's settings: 1800×945px.

**Vertical (4:5 portrait):** Instagram's spec is 864×1080px or 1080×1350px for photos and video.

**Instagram Stories (9:16 tall):** 1080×1920px at 72 DPI for photos, 1920×1080px H.264 for video.
