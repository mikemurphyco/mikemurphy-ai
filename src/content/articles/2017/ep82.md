**Episode Summary:** Part 1 of a 2-part series on building a personal brand — what a personal brand actually is, and the 5 core principles behind building one from scratch.

## What You'll Learn

- What a personal brand actually is, beyond the Wikipedia definition
- Why starting before you feel ready matters more than any other single factor
- The 5 core principles behind building a personal brand
- Why teaching and sharing knowledge builds authority faster than almost anything else

> "Choose a job you love and you will never have to work a day in your life." — attributed to Confucius

This is Part 1 of a two-part series: today covers what a personal brand is and the core principles behind building one; [Part 2](/podcast/ep83/) covers the exact strategies and processes used to build the Mike Murphy brand specifically.

## What Is a Personal Brand?

Per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_branding): "Personal branding is the practice of people marketing themselves and their careers as brands... the ongoing process of establishing a prescribed image or impression in the mind of others about an individual, group, or organization."

**In simpler terms:** a personal brand is building a business around yourself. Showing up and being genuinely you is the whole requirement — building a living around your own skills and uniqueness, doing the work you actually love.

> "Your brand is what other people say about you when you're not in the room." — Jeff Bezos

## How to Build a Personal Brand

**Start yesterday.** Put yourself out there — press upload, press publish. Let people into the process and the journey. Earlier is always better. Worth revisiting: Gary Vaynerchuk's [Document vs. Create](https://www.garyvaynerchuk.com/creating-content-that-builds-your-personal-brand/) — building a brand is fundamentally about community, relationships, and trust, and the more people see what makes you genuinely different, the stronger the brand grows.

**Develop your skills.** A strong personal brand isn't a guarantee — it has to be earned against a lot of competition chasing the same attention. That means reading, watching tutorials, and practicing relentlessly.

**Create often and with consistency.** Sharing what makes you distinctly you — voice, style, signature approach. Daily creation builds real self-awareness over time: what you're good at, what you're not, and what actually makes you happy. When unsure what to make, try everything even remotely interesting and just start making things.

**Treat it like a business.** Personal branding has a genuine operational side — time and money both need managing.

**Teach or share what you know.** The more knowledge shared, the more you're perceived as an authority — and in a crowded, noisy space, being seen as a trusted resource is one of the most reliable ways to stand out.

## Wrap-Up

Personal branding is the ongoing process of marketing yourself and your work as a business brand.

**To build one:**

- **Start as soon as possible.** People are drawn to the behind-the-scenes view — don't wait until every piece is in place.
- **Practice and hard work.** Real skill is what rises above the noise.
- **Create often and build a body of work.** More creation, more consistency, more people paying attention.
- **Get organized and hone business skills.**
- **Teach.** Sharing knowledge builds trust and authority, and confidence follows from helping others.
- **Time.** None of this happens overnight — it takes real time to build.
