**Episode Summary:** A full inventory of the gear, Mac software, and iOS apps behind every video Mike makes — camera and audio hardware, the editing stack, and where the music comes from.

## What You'll Learn

- The 4 main types of video Mike makes: quick tips, gear demos, marketing animations, and vlogs
- The full hardware kit: cameras, mics, lights, tripods, and power
- The complete Mac software and Adobe Creative Cloud stack used for editing
- iOS apps and music/soundtrack sources, free and paid

The most common questions received: what's the favorite tool for making videos, and what software gets used to edit them? The honest answer — an array of gear and programs across Mac and iPhone, chosen based on what each specific video actually needs. Keeping things simple is always the goal, but there's genuinely a lot that goes into video production.

Follow along: [Instagram @mikeunplugged](http://instagram.com/mikemurphyai), [YouTube](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/youtube), and the [full gear list on Kit](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/amazon).

## The 4 Main Types of Video

- **Daily quick tips & tutorials** — posted daily to Instagram and YouTube.
- **Gear & how-to videos** — demos and "Podshed" style walkthroughs.
- **Animations for marketing & products** — weekly podcast promos, the holiday gift guide, and product videos for items like t-shirts and social ads.
- **Vlog & on-location videos** — Instagram Stories–driven promo content.

## Gear Used

- **Computer/devices:** MacBook Pro, iPhone 6 or iPad Pro 9.7".
- **Webcam:** [Logitech C920](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/c920).
- **Microphone:** [Shure SM7B](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/sm7b), mounted on a [Heil Mini Boom Arm](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/heilboom) or a [Manfrotto friction arm](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/manfrottoflex) to keep it above eye level and off the desk.
- **Lighting:** [iKan LED light (main)](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/ikan), [iKan Piatto LED light (fill)](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/ikanpl60).
- **Camera:** [Canon T5i](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/t5i/).
- **Field audio:** [Rode VideoMicro](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/rodemicro), [Rodelink Filmmaker Kit lavalier mic](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/rodelinkreceiver), [Zoom H6](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/zoomh6), [Rode NTG3 shotgun mic](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/ntg3).
- **Tripods & stabilizers:** [MeFoto DayTrip](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/daytrip) (DSLR), [Joby GorillaPod](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/joby), [Manfrotto Pixi Mini](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/pixi), [MeFoto Sidekick 360](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/sidekick) (iPhone), [DJI Osmo](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/osmomobile).
- **Power:** [Eneloop AA rechargeable batteries](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/eneloop), [Anker portable chargers (large](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/ankerlarge) / [small)](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/ankersmall).

## Software Used

[ScreenFlow](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/screenflow), [Camtasia](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/camtasia), the [Webcam Settings app](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/webcamsettings) ($7.99), and AirDrop for moving files from Mac to iPhone.

**Adobe Creative Cloud** ([free trial](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/adobe)): [Photoshop](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/adobe), [Premiere Pro](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html) (video editing), [After Effects](https://www.adobe.com/products/aftereffects.html) (motion/effects), [Audition](https://www.adobe.com/products/audition.html) (audio), [Media Encoder](https://www.adobe.com/products/media-encoder.html) (exporting).

## iOS Tools

[Adobe Draw](http://www.adobe.com/products/draw.html), Adobe Clip, [Adobe Capture](https://www.adobe.com/products/capture.html) (for Looks), [Apple Clips](https://www.apple.com/clips), [Filmora](https://www.wondershare.net/Filmora), [iMovie](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/imovie/id377298193?mt=8), [Filmic Pro](https://www.filmicpro.com/apps/apple/filmic-pro/).

## Music & Soundtracks

**Paid:** [Artlist.io](http://www.artlist.io) ($199/year). **Free:** [YouTube Creator Library](https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary/music), [Freesound.org](https://Freesound.org), [Bensound](https://Bensound.com). **Premium picks:** [PremiumBeat](https://PremiumBeat.com), [Pond5](https://Pond5.com), [Epidemic Sound](https://EpidemicSound.com).
