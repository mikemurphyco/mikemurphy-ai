**Episode Summary:** Part 1 of a 3-part self-publishing series — how to write and edit a book before it ever touches Amazon, including the writing-accountability tools and editing software that make the process manageable.

## What You'll Learn

- The 4-step overview of self-publishing a book on Amazon
- 750Words.com as a daily writing-accountability tool
- 3 recommended writing tools: Scrivener, Ulysses, and Bear
- Editing and grammar tools worth using before anything gets published

Publishing a book was one of the main goals set in [Episode 70: The 2017 Roadmap Show](/podcast/ep70/).

## Overview: How to Self-Publish a Book or eBook on Amazon

Start writing. Edit and format for digital and print (Kindle, iPad, Nook, and others). Upload to an online marketplace like Amazon. Market, promote, and sell.

## Start Writing

Like everything worth accomplishing, the key is simply starting. *"The journey of a thousand miles begins with one step."*

**Tool 1 — [750Words.com](http://750words.com).** A free, simple site built around a daily pledge to write 750 words in a plain browser-based tool — genuinely useful for accountability, building the writing habit, and flushing out new ideas.

## Writing Tools

- **[Workflowy](http://workflowy.com)** — simple outlining tool, good for lesson plans and structure.
- **[Scrivener](http://mikemurphy.co/scrivener)** <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass --> — the main writing and organization tool used.
- **[Ulysses](https://www.ulyssesapp.com/)** — a popular alternative, $44 for Mac plus $25 for the iOS app.

**Note-taking apps for writing:** [Evernote](https://evernote.com/) (no longer in regular use), and [Bear](https://www.bear-writer.com) — a free, well-designed writing app for Mac and iOS that's become a genuine favorite.

**The three recommended writing resources to start with:** [Scrivener](http://mikemurphy.co/scrivener) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->, [Ulysses](https://www.ulyssesapp.com/), and [Bear](https://www.bear-writer.com).

## Editing & Grammar

Once the writing itself is flowing, cleaning up style and grammar matters. [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) is a free Chrome extension (with a $12/month premium tier) that catches readability and grammar issues in real time. [Hemingway App](https://www.hemingwayapp.com/) is a style checker — free as a web app, $20 for the desktop/iOS version.

## Steps to Self-Publishing (Overview)

1. Start writing.
2. Edit and format for digital and print.
3. Upload to an online marketplace like Amazon Kindle and others.
4. Market, promote, and sell.

[Part 2](/podcast/ep73/) covers the actual Amazon self-publishing process, and [Part 3](/podcast/ep74/) covers formatting and distribution beyond Amazon.
