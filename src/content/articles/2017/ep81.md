**Episode Summary:** The content creator blueprint — the 3 types of content you can actually make, real examples of each, and exactly where to publish them once they're finished.

## What You'll Learn

- The 3 core content types: audio, video, and writing
- 10+ concrete examples of video content alone, from Q&As to livestreaming
- Where each content type actually gets distributed, platform by platform
- The difference between content creation and content marketing

**Goal of this episode:** see the big picture of content creation clearly enough to make smarter, more strategic decisions about what to make and where to put it.

**Content creation** is simply the act of making digital content to share or publish online. **Content marketing** is the strategy of using that content to promote a brand, product, or service — built on the idea that valuable content earns relationships and trust over time.

## Part 1: What Type of Content Can You Make?

Audio, video, and writing.

## Part 2: Examples of Each Type

**Audio:** podcasting, audiobooks, audio snippets or voice recordings.

**Video:** behind-the-scenes footage, Q&A videos (live or recorded), about-me/talking-head videos, documentary-style promo videos, tutorials and explainer videos, sellable classes or courses, webinars, gear reviews and product demos, daily vlogging, and web broadcasting/livestreaming.

**Writing:** blogging, books, email newsletters, and white-paper articles/how-tos.

## Part 3: Where Does the Content Go?

**Audio:**

- **Podcasting:** iTunes, Spreaker, Google Play, TuneIn, Stitcher, SoundCloud.
- **Audiobooks:** [ACX (Amazon Creation Exchange)](http://acx.com).
- **Audio snippets:** [Clammr](http://clammr.com), [Anchor](https://anchor.fm), [Wavve](https://wavve.video/).

**Video:** website or blog, YouTube, [Wistia](http://wistia.com), and social media channels.

**Writing:**

- **Blog:** guest posts on outlets like HuffPost or Forbes.
- **White papers/how-tos:** [Medium.com](https://Medium.com) and LinkedIn Pulse.
- **Books:** [Amazon Kindle Publishing](https://kdp.amazon.com/).
- **Email newsletters:** [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com), [AWeber](http://aweber.com), [ConvertKit](https://convertkit.co/).
