**Episode Summary:** A complete resource list for building a brand from scratch — logos, fonts, colors, graphics, stock photos, video footage, music, and where to outsource the rest.

## What You'll Learn

- A complete resource list across branding, photos, video, audio, and outsourcing
- Where to find logos, fonts, and color palettes without hiring a designer
- Free and paid stock resources for photos, video footage, and music
- The top outsourcing platforms for getting things done fast

## The 5 Categories

1. Branding (the big category)
2. Photos (stock photos)
3. Video footage & animations (intros, lower thirds)
4. Audio (for podcasts and video projects)
5. Outsourcing

## 1. Branding & Graphics

Covers logos, fonts, colors, and general graphics.

**Logos:** [Tailor Brands](http://tailorbrands.com) (~$50), [Squarespace Logo](http://www.squarespace.com/logo/) ($10), [99designs](http://99designs.com), [48HourLogo](http://48hourlogo.com). **Bonus tip:** browse [Dribbble](http://dribbble.com) or [LogoPond](http://logopond.com) for logo inspiration.

**Fonts:** [Adobe Typekit](https://typekit.co/foundries/adobe), [MyFonts](http://myfonts.com), [Font Squirrel](http://fontsquirrel.com) (favorite free source), [League of Moveable Type](https://www.theleagueofmoveabletype.com/) (for a hipster fix), [Google Fonts](http://www.google.com/fonts), [DaFont](http://dafont.com). **Pairing fonts:** [Type Genius](http://typegenius.com). **Bonus resource:** a roundup of 100 free fonts from 2015. <!-- REVIEW: original goo.gl shortlink to this roundup is dead — Google shut down goo.gl link creation/redirects; flagged for the link migration pass -->

**Colors:** [Adobe Color CC](http://color.adobe.com) (formerly Kuler), [Coolors](http://coolors.co), and the "super secret" tool [0to255](http://0to255.com) — great for getting fast variations of a color you like.

**Graphics & templates:** the Adobe CC Marketplace (tons of free assets bundled into every CC app), [Adobe Exchange](http://www.adobe.com/mena_en/exchange/), [Creative Market](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/creativemarket), [GraphicRiver](http://graphicriver.net), [Canva](http://canva.com). **Bonus tip:** Canva is also a great place to scope out trendy new fonts.

![Creative Market](/assets/media/2015/08/300x250-E-300x250.jpg)

**Warning:** with any stock resource, respect copyright. Don't just Google a photo and use it because it works — using only photos you have the right to use is your responsibility, and citing the source or pleading ignorance won't cut it.

## 2. Photos

[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/) (hipster favorite), [Death to Stock Photo](http://deathtothestockphoto.com/), [picjumbo](https://picjumbo.com/), [CompFight](http://compfight.com/) (Flickr search). **Bonus resources:** a roundup of 74 places for free photos, and a "great stock sites" list from Fizzle.co. <!-- REVIEW: original goo.gl shortlink for the Fizzle stock-sites roundup is dead — Google shut down goo.gl link creation/redirects; flagged for the link migration pass -->

## 3. Videos

[VideoHive](http://videohive.com) and [VideoBlocks](http://videoblocks.com).

## 4. Audio

Podcast intro music comes from a long-time favorite: [Incompetech](http://incompetech.com) by Kevin MacLeod. Also worth knowing: [Pond5](http://pond5.com), [PremiumBeat](http://premiumbeat.com), [AudioBlocks](http://audioblocks.com), [Marmoset Music](https://www.marmosetmusic.com). **Super secret resource:** the [YouTube Audio Library](https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary/music).

## 5. Outsourcing

The number-one place to get things done, without a doubt: [Fiverr](http://fiverr.com). Also worth checking out: [Upwork](http://upwork.com), [Fancy Hands](http://fancyhands.com), [Freelancer](http://freelancer.com).

Music: "Life of Riley" & "Wallpaper" (incompetech.com).
