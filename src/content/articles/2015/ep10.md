**Episode Summary:** The full 5-step, start-to-finish workflow used to produce one episode of this podcast — planning, scripting, production, show notes, and newsletter — with real time estimates for each.

## What You'll Learn

- The full 5-step production workflow for producing one episode, start to finish
- A real pre-record checklist for cleaner audio
- Roughly how long each production step actually takes
- The tools used at each stage, from scripting to newsletter

## The 5-Step Workflow

1. Planning & pre-production
2. Scripting (writing)
3. Production (recording, editing, tagging, exporting as MP3, uploading to the media server)
4. Web & social media (show notes, blog post, artwork, Buffer scheduling)
5. Newsletter & corresponding with listeners

## Step 1: Planning & Pre-Production (2 hours)

Internet research, "serving hours" (answering and asking questions on social media), listening and learning, and figuring out what people actually need help with. **Resources:** [Pocket](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/pocket/id568494494?mt=12) for web clippings, [Evernote](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/evernote/id406056744?mt=12), and Transpose.

## Step 2: Scripting (4 hours)

**Benefits of scripting:** keeps delivery on point with fewer "umms," speeds up recording, self-educates and sharpens teaching ability, speeds up show notes writing, and grows a content library that pays off later. **Resource:** [Scrivener](http://mikemurphy.co/scrivener) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass -->, the writing app used and recommended for every script.

## Step 3: Production (1.5 hours)

**Steps:** recording & editing, exporting the final mix as MP3, ID3 tagging, uploading to Libsyn (the media server used at the time).

**Pre-record checklist:**

1. Record at the quietest times possible — no outside noise
2. Turn off the A/C and other appliances
3. Add blankets or pillows to dampen an echo-heavy room
4. Clean the desk — get in a clutter-free mindset
5. Have water on the desk
6. Green apples help the voice
7. Set up a GoPro or camera if behind-the-scenes footage is wanted
8. Put on headphones, turn down external speakers
9. Open the script in [Scrivener](http://mikemurphy.co/scrivener) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass --> and [Adobe Audition](http://www.adobe.com/products/audition/features.html)

**Recording & editing (1.5 hours):** [Adobe Audition CC](http://www.adobe.com/products/audition/features.html) is the tool of choice, though GarageBand or Audacity work well too. **Tagging (5 minutes):** [ID3 Editor](http://www.pa-software.com/id3editor/) <!-- REVIEW: pa-software.com — small/old third-party tool, verify still active during the link review pass -->. **Upload (10 minutes):** export as MP3 and upload to [Libsyn](http://libsyn.com) <!-- REVIEW: historical hosting reference — the podcast is hosted on Buzzsprout today -->.

## Step 4: Show Notes & Blog (1.5 hours)

Covers show notes, the blog post, artwork, and Buffer social media scheduling.

1. **Show notes.** A summary of the episode, including links and references to everything mentioned.
2. **Blog post.** The [Divi theme by Elegant Themes](http://mikemurphy.co/divi) <!-- REVIEW: mikemurphy.co Pretty Link, deferred to the Pretty Links → Shlinks migration pass --> makes saving reusable blog post templates easy.
3. **Artwork.** A Photoshop template where only the text needs to change each week.
4. **Schedule.** Posts get scheduled in WordPress to go out Friday at midnight.

## Step 5: Newsletter & Social (30 minutes)

**Newsletter:** [Mailchimp](http://mailchimp.com) is the email newsletter provider. **Social media:** Buffer handles scheduling.

Music: "Life of Riley" & "Wallpaper" (incompetech.com).
