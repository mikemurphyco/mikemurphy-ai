**Episode Summary:** 10 social media strategies in active use — why "go where your audience is" beats trying to be everywhere, and the exact platforms, pages, and handles used to build an audience.

## What You'll Learn

- 10 social media platforms in active rotation, and why each one matters
- Why "go where your audience is" beats trying to be everywhere at once
- The exact handles and pages used across every platform
- A featured podcaster's show and website worth checking out

## Ask Mike Anything: Featured Guest

**Cara Lumen's podcast:** [Reflections of a Deeper Song](http://passionatelyonpurpose.com/series/reflections-deeper-song/feed/). **Cara Lumen's website:** [Passionately on Purpose](http://passionatelyonpurpose.com/).

3. **[Instagram](https://instagram.com/mikeunplugged).**
4. **[YouTube](https://mikemurphy.co/youtube) & Google+.**
5. **[Twitter](http://twitter.com/mikeunplugged).**
6. **[Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com/mikemurphyco/).**
7. **[Periscope](https://itunes.apple.com/app/id972909677).**
8. **Blab.im.**
9. **[Medium](https://medium.com/@mikemurphyco).**
