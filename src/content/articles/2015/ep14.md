**Episode Summary:** Part 1 of the Learn, Create, Move Forward trilogy — a curated tour of every platform and YouTube channel used to keep learning about tech, podcasting, business, design, photography, and video.

## What You'll Learn

- The paid platforms used most for ongoing learning: Lynda, KelbyOne, Skillshare
- A massive curated list of audio, video, photo, and design learning resources
- The real downside of learning too much without practicing or shipping
- Two Skillshare classes taught, for anyone wanting a peek behind the curtain

This episode kicks off a 3-part series: Learn, Create, Move Forward. [Part 2](/podcast/ep15/) covers creation, [Part 3](/podcast/ep16/) covers moving forward.

## Ask Mike Anything: Teaching Podcasting to Students

**Question (from Blab):** From a high school teacher — are there any good resources online to teach students about podcasting?

**Answer:** [The Free Podcast Course by John Lee Dumas](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/free-podcast-course-podcast/id947671706?mt=2), and this show itself, [Mike Murphy Unplugged](https://go.mikemurphy.ai/itunes).

## The Cons of Learning Too Much

Becoming a jack-of-all-trades, losing track of what you're actually good at, not practicing what's being learned, and fear of ever actually starting something.

## Where I Learn

[Lynda.com](http://lynda.com/) ($35/month) — the best, hands down. [KelbyOne](http://kelbyone.com/) ($199/year) — photography, Photoshop, video, and more. [Skillshare](http://skillshare.com/) ($9.99/month).

![Lynda.com](/assets/media/2015/08/Lynda.com_-150x150.png)

**Favorite Skillshare classes:** [Logo Design the Draplin Way: Building with Shape, Type, and Color](https://www.skillshare.com/classes/design/Logo-Design-the-Draplin-Way-Building-with-Shape-Type-and-Color/1389209398?via=user-profile) by Aaron Draplin, and [Buy Smarter, Taste More: Getting Started with Wine](https://www.skillshare.com/classes/culinary/Buy-Smarter-Taste-More-Getting-Started-with-Wine/855277635?via=search-layout-grid) by Gary Vaynerchuk.

**Classes taught on Skillshare:** [Photoshop: The Art of the Headswap](https://www.skillshare.com/classes/photography/Photoshop-The-Art-of-the-Head-Swap/1700917171) and [WordPress Cheatsheet for Entrepreneurs](https://www.skillshare.com/classes/technology/Wordpress-For-Entrepreneurs-The-Quick-Easy-Install-Cheatsheet/1105132078).
